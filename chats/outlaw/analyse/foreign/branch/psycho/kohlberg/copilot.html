好的。我将结合劳伦斯·科尔伯格（Lawrence Kohlberg）的道德发展阶段理论（Theory of Moral Development）——即从前习俗水平（pre-conventional）、习俗水平（conventional）到后习俗水平（post-conventional） 的六个阶段——来分析陈京元案。以下为中英文对照的评析：

一、前习俗水平：服从与惩罚

Pre-Conventional Level: Obedience and Punishment

中文： 在最初阶段，道德判断基于避免惩罚。在本案中，国家通过“寻衅滋事”的惩罚逻辑，实际上是将公民的行为压制在前习俗水平：人们因害怕惩罚而不敢表达，而不是出于对公共善的理解。

English: At the earliest stage, moral judgment is based on avoiding punishment. In this case, the state’s use of the charge of “picking quarrels” effectively forced citizens into the pre-conventional level: people refrained from expression out of fear of punishment, not from an understanding of the common good.

二、习俗水平：法律与秩序

Conventional Level: Law and Order

中文： 在习俗水平，道德判断依赖于遵守法律与维护社会秩序。国家强调“秩序”与“稳定”，正是典型的习俗水平思维。然而，科尔伯格指出，单纯的法律服从并不足以代表真正的道德成熟，因为法律本身可能是不公正的。

English: At the conventional level, moral judgment relies on adherence to law and maintenance of social order. The state’s emphasis on “order” and “stability” reflects this stage. Yet Kohlberg noted that mere obedience to law does not represent true moral maturity, since laws themselves may be unjust.

三、后习俗水平：社会契约与普遍原则

Post-Conventional Level: Social Contract and Universal Principles

中文： 在更高的发展阶段，个体会超越法律本身，依据社会契约与普遍的道德原则来判断。在本案中，陈京元通过言论表达对真理与正义的追求，体现了后习俗水平的道德立场：他将自由、真相与公共责任置于法律惩罚之上。这种行为正是科尔伯格所说的“普遍伦理原则”阶段的体现。

English: At higher stages, individuals transcend the law itself, judging according to social contracts and universal moral principles. In this case, Chen’s speech expressed a pursuit of truth and justice, reflecting a post-conventional moral stance: he placed freedom, truth, and civic responsibility above legal punishment. This exemplifies what Kohlberg called the “universal ethical principles” stage.

四、冲突的根源

The Root of the Conflict

中文： 本案的冲突在于：国家的道德逻辑停留在习俗水平（强调秩序与服从），而个体的道德实践已进入后习俗水平（强调普遍正义与自由）。这种“道德发展阶段的不对称”导致了制度与个人之间的对抗。

English: The conflict in this case lies in the fact that the state’s moral logic remains at the conventional level (emphasizing order and obedience), while the individual’s moral practice has reached the post-conventional level (emphasizing universal justice and freedom). This “asymmetry of moral development stages” produced the clash between institution and individual.

五、结论

Conclusion

中文：从科尔伯格的道德发展理论视角看，陈京元案揭示了：

  • 国家通过惩罚将公民压制在前习俗或习俗水平；

  • 个体的道德选择体现了后习俗水平的普遍原则；

  • 冲突的根源在于不同道德发展阶段的错位；

  • 真正的道德成熟要求社会制度能够承认并保护后习俗水平的价值。

科尔伯格会提醒我们：一个公正的社会，不应仅仅要求服从法律，而应当鼓励公民基于普遍正义与人类尊严来行动。

English:From Kohlberg’s moral development perspective, Chen Jingyuan’s case reveals that:

  • The state’s punishment suppressed citizens at the pre- or conventional level.

  • The individual’s moral choice reflected post-conventional universal principles.

  • The root of conflict lies in the mismatch of moral development stages.

  • True moral maturity requires institutions to recognize and protect post-conventional values.

Kohlberg would remind us that a just society should not merely demand obedience to law but encourage citizens to act on universal justice and human dignity.
