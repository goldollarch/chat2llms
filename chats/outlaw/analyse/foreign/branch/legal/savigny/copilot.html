核心观点 / Core Insight
中文：从萨维尼的历史法学派视角看，陈京元案暴露了法律与社会“民族精神”之间的断裂。司法系统未能体现法律的历史演进与社会共识，而是以抽象条文压制个体表达，违背了法律应源于人民生活的基本原则。
English: From Savigny’s Historical School of Law, the Chen Jingyuan case reveals a rupture between law and the “Volksgeist” (national spirit). The legal system failed to reflect the historical evolution and social consensus of law, instead using abstract statutes to suppress individual expression—violating the principle that law must arise organically from the life of the people.

一、萨维尼核心思想概述

Overview of Savigny’s Core Ideas

  • 中文：萨维尼认为法律不是由立法者凭空制定，而是源于民族精神（Volksgeist），即一个民族在历史中形成的语言、习俗、信仰与法律意识。

  • English: Savigny argued that law is not arbitrarily created by legislators but emerges from the Volksgeist—the collective spirit formed through a people’s history, language, customs, and legal consciousness.

  • 中文：他反对抽象的自然法与激进立法，主张法律应通过历史积淀与法学家的学术加工逐步发展。

  • English: He opposed abstract natural law and radical legislation, advocating that law should evolve gradually through historical accumulation and scholarly refinement.

  • 中文：法律的正当性来自于其与社会生活的有机联系，而非强制性条文。

  • English: Legal legitimacy stems from its organic connection to social life, not from coercive statutes.

二、案件分析 Case Analysis

1. 法律与民族精神的脱节 / Disconnection from Volksgeist

  • 中文：本案中，“寻衅滋事”作为定罪依据，缺乏与社会习俗和公共认知的联系，未体现法律的历史根基。

  • English: The use of “picking quarrels” as a charge lacks connection to social customs and public understanding, failing to reflect law’s historical foundations.

2. 法律形式主义的危害 / Harm of Legal Formalism

  • 中文：萨维尼警惕法律形式主义可能割裂法律与现实生活。本案以学历推定“明知”，以转发定性“扰乱”，显示出法律与社会经验的脱节。

  • English: Savigny warned that legal formalism could sever law from real life. This case’s presumption of intent based on education and its criminalization of reposting show a disconnect from lived social experience.

3. 法学家的责任缺失 / Absence of Jurist Mediation

  • 中文：历史法学派强调法学家应作为民族法律意识的代表，解释并引导法律发展。本案中，司法未体现对社会法意识的尊重，缺乏法学家的理性调和。

  • English: The Historical School saw jurists as mediators of legal consciousness. In this case, the judiciary failed to respect societal legal awareness, lacking rational mediation by legal scholars.

三、结语与启示

Conclusion and Reflections

  • 中文：萨维尼提醒我们，法律应扎根于民族生活与历史传统。陈京元案的处理方式显示出法律与社会之间的断裂，违背了历史法学派“法律源于人民”的基本理念。唯有重建法律与社会之间的有机联系，司法才能获得真正的合法性与尊重。

  • English: Savigny reminds us that law must be rooted in the life and historical traditions of the people. The handling of Chen’s case reveals a rupture between law and society, violating the Historical School’s core belief that law arises from the people. Only by restoring this organic connection can justice regain legitimacy and public respect.
