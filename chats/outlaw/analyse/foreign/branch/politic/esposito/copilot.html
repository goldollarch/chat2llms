好的。我将结合罗伯托·埃斯波西托（Roberto Esposito）的独特“生命政治”（biopolitics）思想——尤其是他提出的 免疫（immunitas）与共同体（communitas） 的张力——来分析陈京元案。以下为中英文对照的评析：

一、免疫逻辑与国家权力

Immunitary Logic and State Power

中文： 埃斯波西托认为，现代政治的核心是“免疫逻辑”：国家通过排除、限制或牺牲部分生命来保护整体秩序。在陈京元案中，国家将一位知识分子的转发行为视为“病毒”般的威胁，以“寻衅滋事”之名将其隔离、惩罚。这正是免疫机制的体现：通过牺牲个体的自由来维持所谓的“公共秩序”。

English: Esposito argues that the core of modern politics is the “immunitary logic”: the state protects the whole by excluding, restricting, or sacrificing certain lives. In Chen Jingyuan’s case, the state treated a scholar’s reposting of messages as a viral threat, isolating and punishing him under the charge of “picking quarrels.” This is precisely the immunitary mechanism: sacrificing individual freedom to preserve so-called “public order.”

二、共同体的丧失

The Loss of Community

中文： 在埃斯波西托的框架中，“共同体”意味着共享（munus），即人与人之间的责任与开放性。然而，当免疫逻辑被极端化时，共同体被瓦解，社会成员之间不再是互相承担，而是互相隔离。本案中，国家没有将陈京元的言论视为公共讨论的一部分，而是将其排除为“危险他者”。这破坏了共同体赖以存在的共享空间。

English: In Esposito’s framework, “community” means munus—a relation of sharing and responsibility among people. Yet when the immunitary logic is radicalized, community disintegrates: members are no longer bound by mutual responsibility but by mutual exclusion. In this case, the state did not treat Chen’s speech as part of public discourse but excluded him as a “dangerous Other.” This undermines the shared space upon which community depends.

三、生命政治的悖论

The Paradox of Biopolitics

中文： 埃斯波西托指出，生命政治的悖论在于：它以保护生命为名，却常常通过限制、剥夺甚至毁灭生命来实现。在本案中，国家声称要维护“公共秩序”，但这种保护是通过剥夺公民的基本自由来完成的。结果是，生命政治转化为“反生命政治”：它不再滋养共同体，而是制造恐惧与孤立。

English: Esposito highlights the paradox of biopolitics: in the name of protecting life, it often restricts, deprives, or even destroys life. In this case, the state claimed to safeguard “public order,” but this protection was achieved by stripping citizens of basic freedoms. The result is that biopolitics turns into thanatopolitics: instead of nourishing community, it produces fear and isolation.

四、免疫与共同体的再平衡

Rebalancing Immunity and Community

中文： 埃斯波西托的哲学启示在于：免疫机制并非完全负面，它在一定程度上保护共同体免于崩溃。但若免疫逻辑压倒一切，共同体就会丧失。陈京元案提醒我们，真正的政治应当在免疫与共同体之间找到平衡：既保障社会秩序，又不牺牲个体的自由与尊严。

English: Esposito’s insight is that immunity is not wholly negative—it protects the community from collapse. But when immunitary logic dominates everything, community is lost. Chen’s case reminds us that true politics must balance immunity and community: safeguarding social order without sacrificing individual freedom and dignity.

五、结论

Conclusion

中文： 从埃斯波西托的视角看，陈京元案是生命政治免疫逻辑的极端化体现：国家以“保护”为名，将异议者排除为威胁，从而破坏了共同体的共享与信任。真正的政治应当超越这种“反生命政治”，重建一个既能保障秩序又能容纳多元声音的共同体。

English: From Esposito’s perspective, Chen Jingyuan’s case exemplifies the radicalization of the immunitary logic of biopolitics: the state, in the name of “protection,” excludes dissenters as threats, thereby undermining the sharing and trust of community. True politics must transcend this thanatopolitical turn and rebuild a community that safeguards order while embracing plural voices.
