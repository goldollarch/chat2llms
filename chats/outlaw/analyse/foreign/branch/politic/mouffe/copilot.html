激进民主核心要义概述 | Core Tenets of Radical Democracy

中文：
尚塔尔·墨菲主张政治不可消弭的对立性，提出“冲突不可消除但可民主化”的命题，强调在多元对抗中维护公共空间的争论性与对手性；她反对将政治冲突技术化、管理化或话语僵化化，主张通过制度与民主实践把敌对关系转为可公开竞争的政治场域，从而防止对抗被封锁为非政治的刑事或行政话语。

English:
Chantal Mouffe argues that political antagonism is ineradicable and must be democratized. She insists on preserving the agonistic public space where adversaries contest; she opposes technocratic management or juridification that depoliticizes conflict and transforms political antagonism into non‑political criminal or administrative language.

公共对手性与案件的基本张力 | Public Agonism and the Case’s Fundamental Tension

中文：
此案将学术与政治性表达的对立通过刑事化机制封闭为“安全/秩序”问题，移除了对手性作为可争论的政治维度，从而把政治争论转化为单向的权力制裁。墨菲会认为这是把政治对抗“去政治化”的操作，削弱了民主的争论空间。

English:
The case criminalizes academic and political expression by framing conflict as a “security/order” issue, removing agonistic contestation as a political dimension and converting political debate into unilateral power sanction. Mouffe would identify this as depoliticization that weakens democratic contestation.

冲突民主化被替代为刑罚逻辑 | Democratization of Conflict Replaced by Penal Logic

中文：
墨菲强调应把对立转为合法的政治抗争和制度竞争。本案以秘密程序、标签化指控和剥夺辩护的方式处理异议，是以刑罚逻辑替代民主化路径，导致对立成为被消音的敌意，而非公共辩论的动力。

English:
Mouffe stresses turning antagonism into legitimate political struggle and institutional competition. The case’s use of secret procedures, labeling, and denial of defense substitutes penal logic for democratization, silencing adversariality instead of channeling it into public debate.

公共话语的收缩与民主正当性的侵蚀 | Contraction of Public Discourse and Erosion of Democratic Legitimacy

中文：
压制具有争议性的话语会使公共话语收缩并产生寒蝉效应，墨菲认为这种收缩削弱了民主的正当性，使政治代理关系退化为单向的治理命令而非具有竞争性的代表性实践。

English:
Suppressing controversial speech contracts public discourse and produces a chilling effect; Mouffe holds that this contraction undermines democratic legitimacy, reducing political representation to one‑way governance rather than competitive representative practice.

政策性修复路径 | Remedial Paths

中文：

  • 恢复争论性公共空间：通过透明审判、保障辩护与公开对话把对手性带回政治场域。

  • 防止去政治化的话语转化：把应对争议的问题置于政治制度与公共辩论，而非直接以刑罚或安全名义处置。

  • 制度化对抗渠道：建立包容但对抗的制度渠道，使异议能在制度内转化为可竞争的政治诉求。

English:

  • Restore agonistic public space: Reinstate transparent trials, guarantee defense, and enable public dialogue to return antagonism to the political sphere.

  • Prevent depoliticizing conversions: Treat disputes within political institutions and public debate rather than immediate penal or security responses.

  • Institutionalize channels for contestation: Create inclusive but adversarial institutional pathways so dissent can be transformed into contestable political claims.

结论 | Conclusion

中文：
以墨菲的激进民主理论评析，本案体现了对抗被刑罚化与话语被去政治化的趋势，造成公共争论空间萎缩与民主正当性受损。激进民主的修复要求把政治对立带回制度化竞争与公开争论，从而以民主方式承认并管理冲突，而非以权力强制消除异见。

English:
Evaluated by Mouffe’s radical democracy, the case exhibits the penalization of antagonism and depoliticization of discourse, shrinking public debate and eroding democratic legitimacy. Radical democracy demands restoring political oppositions into institutional competition and open contestation, acknowledging and managing conflict democratically rather than eliminating dissent through power.
