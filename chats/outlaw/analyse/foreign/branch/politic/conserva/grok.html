基于保守主义政治哲学核心思想的陈京元“寻衅滋事罪”案分析评论

An Analysis of the Chen Jingyuan “Picking Quarrels and Provoking Trouble” Case Based on the Core Ideas of Conservative Political Philosophy

保守主义政治哲学（Conservatism），以埃德蒙·伯克（Edmund Burke）为奠基人，罗素·柯克（Russell Kirk）为现代阐释者，其核心思想强调社会有机体、尊重传统、谨慎变革与自由秩序的平衡，反对激进主义与抽象乌托邦。 伯克视社会为“世代契约”，变革须审慎，维护习俗与道德想象力；柯克提出十原则，包括信念、习俗、财产与有序自由。 本案中，陈京元博士因X平台转发艺术作品、时政观点等内容（粉丝不足百人、互动近零），被以“寻衅滋事罪”判处有期徒刑一年八个月，程序中充斥主观推定、剥夺自辩与选择性执法。从保守主义视角，此案非维护秩序，而是激进压制破坏社会有机体：违背传统自由、审慎法治，背离保守信念。

Conservative political philosophy, founded by Edmund Burke and elaborated by Russell Kirk, centers on the organic society, respect for tradition, prudent change, and balanced freedom and order, opposing radicalism and abstract utopias. Burke saw society as an “intergenerational contract,” demanding cautious reform to preserve customs and moral imagination; Kirk’s ten principles include belief, custom, property, and ordered liberty. In this case, Dr. Chen Jingyuan, an independent scholar, was sentenced to one year and eight months’ imprisonment for “picking quarrels and provoking trouble” due to forwarding artistic works and political commentary on X (formerly Twitter)—with fewer than 100 followers and near-zero engagement—amid procedural flaws like subjective presumption, denial of self-defense, and selective enforcement. From conservatism’s viewpoint, this is not order’s defense but radical suppression disrupting the organic society: betraying traditional liberty and prudent rule of law, violating conservative tenets.

一、保守主义政治哲学核心思想概述：社会有机体与审慎秩序

I. Overview of Conservative Political Philosophy’s Core Ideas: Organic Society and Prudent Order

保守主义视社会为有机体，非原子个体集合：伯克强调“社会是活的传统”，变革须渐进，破坏习俗即毁秩序。 柯克的六条准则包括神圣意图、习俗第二自然、审慎变革与有序自由。 核心原则：尊重处方（prescription，积累智慧）、道德想象力与财产权利，反对抽象平等与激进平等主义。 保守主义捍卫机构（如法治）以维护自由，但怀疑国家过度干预。

Conservatism views society as an organic body, not an aggregate of atomic individuals: Burke stressed “society is a living tradition,” requiring gradual change to avoid disrupting order. Kirk’s six canons include divine intent, custom as second nature, prudent change, and ordered liberty. Core tenets: respect for prescription (accumulated wisdom), moral imagination, and property rights, opposing abstract equality and radical egalitarianism. Conservatism defends institutions (e.g., rule of law) to preserve liberty but distrusts state overreach.

二、以保守主义政治哲学核心思想评析本案

II. Analysis of the Case Based on Conservative Political Philosophy’s Core Ideas

  1. 激进压制破坏社会有机体：违背审慎变革与传统自由
    保守主义强调渐进变革，尊重传统习俗以维护有机社会。 本案判决将陈京元转发的情感表达（如讽刺帖）、理性观点（如智库报告）与艺术作品（如漫画隐喻）泛化为“虚假言论”，无证据证明社会危害，却激进判“寻衅滋事”，忽略传统言论自由（普通法遗产）。 账号数据显示零互动、无冲突，却被“梳理”为“铁证”，这正是伯克斥的激进主义：抽象“秩序”名义下，破坏社会有机体，扼杀习俗表达。 保守主义若在，必判此不审慎——变革言论法须渐进，非一判倾覆传统自由。

  2. Radical Suppression Disrupts Organic Society: Violating Prudent Change and Traditional Liberty
    Conservatism stresses gradual change, respecting customs to preserve the organic society. The judgment categorizes Dr. Chen’s forwarded emotional expressions (e.g., satirical posts), rational opinions (e.g., think tank reports), and artistic works (e.g., metaphorical cartoons) as “false statements,” without evidence of social harm, radically sentencing “picking quarrels,” ignoring traditional free speech (common law heritage). Account data shows zero engagement and no conflicts, yet “collated” as “ironclad evidence”—precisely Burke’s radicalism critique: abstract “order” disrupts the organic society, stifling customary expression. Conservatism would deem this imprudent—speech law reform must be gradual, not upending tradition in one judgment.

  3. 机构滥用与道德想象力缺失：背离有序自由与习俗尊重
    柯克强调有序自由需道德想象力与机构尊重。 陈京元转发系审慎表达（如复杂系统引用），维护社会习俗（学术辩论），却被禁自辩（庭审“闭嘴”）、拒转控告书，程序中“选择性执法”（党媒同类未责）滥用机构，制造恐惧。 这违背保守原则：法治机构须守护自由，非激进工具；缺失道德想象力，失却习俗平衡。 保守主义批判：此案破坏有机体，永固不稳。

  4. Institutional Abuse and Absence of Moral Imagination: Betraying Ordered Liberty and Custom Respect
    Kirk stressed ordered liberty requires moral imagination and institutional respect. Dr. Chen’s forwards represent prudent expression (e.g., complex systems citations), upholding social customs (academic debate), yet he was denied self-defense (courtroom “silencing”), his indictment letter rejected, and selective enforcement abused institutions, breeding fear (state media reposts unpunished). This violates conservative tenets: rule-of-law institutions must guard liberty, not radical tools; lacking moral imagination forfeits customary balance. Conservatism indicts: this disrupts the organic body, entrenching instability.

  5. 处方与财产权利失衡：荒谬警示与社会危机
    保守主义尊处方（积累智慧）与财产（包括知识产权与表达权）。 陈京元Bio自嘲“作案工具”，帖文存网无人关注，却判20月，荒谬如伯克比喻“激进变革毁传统”——处方（言论习俗）被抽象罪名碾压，财产（学术自由）遭侵。 这警示：失衡保守，永固危机。

  6. Imbalanced Prescription and Property Rights: Absurd Warning and Social Crisis
    Conservatism reveres prescription (accumulated wisdom) and property (including intellectual and expressive rights). Dr. Chen’s bio mocks his account as a “tool of crime,” with posts lingering online unheeded, yet earning 20 months—absurd like Burke’s metaphor of “radical change ruining tradition”: prescription (speech customs) crushed by abstract crimes, property (academic liberty) invaded. This warns: imbalanced conservatism entrenches crisis.

三、结语：重振审慎秩序，推动有机新生

III. Conclusion: Reviving Prudent Order for Organic Rebirth

保守主义视本案为悲剧：激进破坏有机，机构滥用失平衡，永固不稳危机。陈京元自辩如伯克审慎呼吁，重审恢复传统自由，方能让社会在习俗中新生。 如伯克所言：“社会是活的传统。”唯有以此衡，方避“激进”成“公敌”，推动人文秩序。

Conservatism sees this case as tragedy: radical disruption of organic society, institutional abuse loses balance, entrenching instability’s crisis. Dr. Chen’s defense echoes Burke’s prudence, urging retrial to restore traditional liberty, allowing society to rebirth in customs. As Burke stated: “Society is a living tradition.” Only thus can we avert “radicalism” as “public enemy,” advancing humanistic order.
