好的。我将结合皮埃尔-约瑟夫·普鲁东（Pierre-Joseph Proudhon）的政治哲学核心思想——尤其是他提出的 “财产即盗窃”（Property is theft）、权威与国家的批判、互助与自我管理、以及正义作为社会关系的平等性——来分析陈京元案。以下为中英文对照的评析：

一、国家与权威的批判

Critique of the State and Authority

中文： 普鲁东认为，国家与权威往往以“公共利益”为名，实则维护特权阶层的利益。在陈京元案中，国家机关以“寻衅滋事”之名惩罚公民的言论，这并不是为了真正的公共秩序，而是为了维护权力结构的稳定。普鲁东会指出，这种做法揭示了国家权威的虚伪性：它并非保护社会，而是压制社会的活力。

English: Proudhon argued that the state and authority often act in the name of the “public good” but in reality serve the interests of privileged classes. In Chen Jingyuan’s case, the state punished speech under the charge of “picking quarrels,” not to preserve genuine public order but to maintain the stability of the power structure. Proudhon would see this as exposing the hypocrisy of state authority: it does not protect society but suppresses its vitality.

二、法律与“盗窃”的逻辑

Law and the Logic of “Theft”

中文： 普鲁东著名地提出“财产即盗窃”，其核心思想是：当财产或权力被少数人垄断时，它就成为对多数人的剥夺。同样，法律若被垄断为统治工具，它也就成了“盗窃”。在本案中，“寻衅滋事罪”的模糊性使得法律成为任意支配的工具，剥夺了公民的自由与尊严。这正是普鲁东意义上的“法律即盗窃”。

English: Proudhon famously declared “property is theft,” meaning that when property or power is monopolized by a few, it becomes a form of dispossession for the many. Similarly, when law is monopolized as a tool of domination, it too becomes “theft.” In this case, the vagueness of the crime of “picking quarrels” turned law into an instrument of arbitrary control, depriving citizens of freedom and dignity. This is law as theft in Proudhon’s sense.

三、互助与社会自我管理

Mutualism and Social Self-Management

中文： 普鲁东主张社会应当建立在互助与自我管理的基础上，而不是依赖国家的强制。在陈京元案中，公民之间的言论交流本可以成为互助与公共理性的体现，但国家却将其视为威胁并加以惩罚。这种做法破坏了社会自我组织的可能性，使公共生活失去了自由发展的空间。

English: Proudhon advocated that society should be based on mutualism and self-management rather than state coercion. In Chen’s case, citizens’ exchange of opinions could have been an expression of mutual aid and public reason, but the state treated it as a threat and punished it. This undermined the possibility of social self-organization and deprived public life of the space for free development.

四、正义与平等的社会关系

Justice and Equality as Social Relations

中文： 普鲁东认为，正义不是抽象的理念，而是人与人之间平等关系的体现。本案中，国家选择性执法，只惩罚特定个体而非所有转发者，这种不平等破坏了社会的正义。普鲁东会指出，真正的正义应当体现在平等的社会关系中，而不是权力对个体的单方面压制。

English: Proudhon saw justice not as an abstract ideal but as the expression of equality in social relations. In this case, the state selectively enforced the law, punishing one individual but not all who reposted. This inequality undermined justice. Proudhon would argue that true justice lies in equal social relations, not in the unilateral suppression of individuals by power.

五、结论

Conclusion

中文：从普鲁东的视角看，陈京元案揭示了：

  • 国家权威以“秩序”为名，实则维护统治；

  • 法律被工具化，成为“盗窃”的形式；

  • 公民的互助与公共理性被压制；

  • 正义因不平等的执法而丧失。

普鲁东会提醒我们：真正的社会应当建立在互助、自我管理与平等关系之上，而不是依赖国家的强制与法律的任意性。

English:From Proudhon’s perspective, Chen Jingyuan’s case reveals that:

  • State authority invoked “order” but in reality preserved domination.

  • Law was instrumentalized, becoming a form of “theft.”

  • Citizens’ mutual aid and public reason were suppressed.

  • Justice was lost through unequal enforcement.

Proudhon would remind us that a genuine society must be built on mutualism, self-management, and equal relations, not on state coercion and arbitrary law.
