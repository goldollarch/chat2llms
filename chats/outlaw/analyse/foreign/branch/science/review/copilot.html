好的。我将结合当代科学哲学（Contemporary Philosophy of Science）的一些核心思想——包括科学的社会建构性（social constructivism）、科学与价值的关系（science and values）、科学实在论与反实在论的争论（realism vs. anti-realism）、以及多元主义与跨学科视角（pluralism & interdisciplinarity）——来分析陈京元案。以下为中英文对照的评析：

一、科学与社会建构

Science and Social Constructivism

中文：
当代科学哲学强调，科学知识并非完全中立，而是与社会制度、权力结构和文化背景交织在一起。在本案中，法律语言（如“寻衅滋事”）并非单纯的事实陈述，而是一种社会建构：它通过权力话语来界定“秩序”与“威胁”。这种建构性揭示了法律并非纯粹理性，而是深受政治与社会力量的影响。

English:
Contemporary philosophy of science emphasizes that scientific knowledge is not entirely neutral but intertwined with institutions, power structures, and cultural contexts. In this case, legal language (such as “picking quarrels”) was not a mere factual description but a social construction: it defined “order” and “threat” through the discourse of power. This constructivist view shows that law is not purely rational but deeply shaped by political and social forces.

二、科学与价值的交织

The Entanglement of Science and Values

中文：
当代科学哲学指出，科学实践不可避免地包含价值判断。在本案中，国家将“稳定”作为最高价值，并据此解释和处理言论。然而，这种价值选择排除了“自由”“正义”等同样重要的社会价值。案件表明，法律与社会判断并非价值中立，而是反映了特定的价值优先序。

English:
Contemporary philosophy of science notes that scientific practice inevitably involves value judgments. In this case, the state prioritized “stability” as the supreme value, interpreting and handling speech accordingly. Yet this choice excluded other equally important values such as “freedom” and “justice.” The case illustrates that legal and social judgments are not value-neutral but reflect specific value hierarchies.

三、实在论与反实在论的争论

Realism vs. Anti-Realism Debate

中文：
当代科学哲学中，实在论者认为理论应揭示真实的因果机制，而反实在论者则强调理论只是“有用的工具”。在本案中，国家的叙事更接近工具主义：它并不追求揭示社会矛盾的真实机制，而是将“扰乱秩序”的标签作为维稳工具。相比之下，异议言论更接近实在论的立场：它试图揭示社会不公这一真实存在的机制。

English:
In contemporary philosophy of science, realists argue that theories should reveal real causal mechanisms, while anti-realists stress that theories are merely “useful instruments.” In this case, the state’s narrative resembled instrumentalism: it did not aim to uncover the real mechanisms of social conflict but used the label of “disturbing order” as a tool of control. By contrast, dissenting speech aligned more with realism: it sought to reveal the genuinely existing mechanisms of injustice.

四、多元主义与跨学科视角

Pluralism and Interdisciplinary Perspectives

中文：
当代科学哲学越来越强调知识的多元性与跨学科性。社会问题不能用单一的框架来解释，而需要多种视角的对话。在本案中，如果社会允许不同声音共存，就能像科学多元主义所强调的那样，通过竞争与对话不断修正自身。然而，国家的压制阻断了这种多元性，使社会知识体系陷入单一化与僵化。

English:
Contemporary philosophy of science increasingly emphasizes pluralism and interdisciplinarity. Social problems cannot be explained by a single framework but require dialogue among multiple perspectives. In this case, if society had allowed diverse voices to coexist, it could, as scientific pluralism suggests, self-correct through competition and dialogue. Yet the state’s repression blocked such pluralism, leaving the social knowledge system monolithic and rigid.

五、结论

Conclusion

中文：
从当代科学哲学的视角看，陈京元案揭示了：

  • 法律语言是一种社会建构，而非中立事实；

  • 社会判断深受价值选择影响；

  • 国家叙事体现了工具主义，而异议更接近实在论的真理追求；

  • 压制多元声音导致社会知识体系退化。

当代科学哲学会提醒我们：一个健康的社会应当像科学一样，承认知识的建构性与价值性，同时保持对真理的追求，并通过多元对话不断修正自身。

English:
From the perspective of contemporary philosophy of science, Chen Jingyuan’s case reveals that:

  • Legal language is a social construction, not a neutral fact.

  • Social judgments are deeply shaped by value choices.

  • The state’s narrative reflected instrumentalism, while dissent aligned more with realism’s pursuit of truth.

  • Suppression of plural voices led to the degeneration of the social knowledge system.

Contemporary philosophy of science would remind us that a healthy society, like science, must acknowledge the constructed and value-laden nature of knowledge, while still pursuing truth and enabling plural dialogue for continuous self-correction.
