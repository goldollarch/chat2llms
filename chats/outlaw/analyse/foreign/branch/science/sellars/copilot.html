好的。我将结合威尔弗雷德·塞拉斯（Wilfrid Sellars）的哲学核心思想——尤其是他提出的**“显现图景（manifest image）与科学图景（scientific image）”的区分，以及他对“神话的空间”（space of reasons）与“因果的空间”（space of causes）**的分析——来评析陈京元案。以下为中英文对照的分析：

一、显现图景与科学图景

Manifest Image and Scientific Image

中文：
塞拉斯区分了“显现图景”（人类日常经验、价值与规范的世界）与“科学图景”（通过科学理论揭示的因果与结构）。在本案中，国家的法律叙事停留在一种“显现图景”的表象：它将批判性言论直接等同于“扰乱秩序”。然而，如果从“科学图景”的角度来看，社会秩序的稳定与否涉及复杂的制度性、历史性与结构性因素，而非单一言论即可决定。国家的做法是用显现图景的表象来掩盖科学图景所揭示的深层因果。

English:
Sellars distinguished between the “manifest image” (the world of everyday experience, values, and norms) and the “scientific image” (the world revealed by scientific theories of causes and structures). In this case, the state’s legal narrative remained at the level of the manifest image: it equated critical speech with “disturbing order.” Yet from the perspective of the scientific image, social stability involves complex institutional, historical, and structural factors, not reducible to a single act of speech. The state’s move was to mask deeper causal realities with a manifest appearance.

二、理由的空间与因果的空间

Space of Reasons and Space of Causes

中文：
塞拉斯强调，人类行动既属于“因果的空间”（自然因果链条），也属于“理由的空间”（规范与理性的解释）。在本案中，陈京元的言论应当被理解为“理由的空间”的产物：他基于理性与道德理由提出批判。然而，国家却将其完全纳入“因果的空间”，把言论视为一种危险的因果因素，而不是理性的理由表达。这种混淆导致了对行动意义的误读。

English:
Sellars emphasized that human actions belong both to the “space of causes” (natural causal chains) and the “space of reasons” (normative and rational explanations). In this case, Chen Jingyuan’s speech should be understood as belonging to the space of reasons: he articulated critique based on rational and moral grounds. Yet the state reduced it entirely to the space of causes, treating speech as a dangerous causal factor rather than a rational expression of reasons. This conflation led to a misinterpretation of the action’s meaning.

三、规范性与社会理性

Normativity and Social Rationality

中文：
塞拉斯认为，真正的人类理解必须整合因果解释与规范理由。在本案中，国家的做法忽视了规范性维度：它没有承认言论作为理性批判的正当性，而是仅仅将其视为扰乱因果链条的“变量”。这种做法削弱了社会的理性基础，使公共领域失去了“理由的空间”。

English:
Sellars argued that genuine human understanding must integrate causal explanations with normative reasons. In this case, the state ignored the normative dimension: it failed to recognize speech as a legitimate act of rational critique, treating it instead as a variable in a causal chain of disorder. This undermined the rational foundation of society, depriving the public sphere of its “space of reasons.”

四、结论

Conclusion

中文：
从塞拉斯的哲学视角看，陈京元案揭示了：

  • 国家用“显现图景”掩盖了“科学图景”的复杂因果；

  • 将理性批判的言论从“理由的空间”错误地降格为“因果的空间”；

  • 忽视了规范性与理性维度，导致社会失去公共理性的基础。

塞拉斯会提醒我们：一个健康的社会必须同时尊重“因果的空间”与“理由的空间”，既要理解社会结构的因果机制，也要承认公民理性批判的规范性意义。

English:
From Sellars’ philosophical perspective, Chen Jingyuan’s case reveals that:

  • The state used the “manifest image” to obscure the deeper causal complexities of the “scientific image.”

  • Rational critical speech was wrongly reduced from the “space of reasons” to the “space of causes.”

  • The normative and rational dimension was ignored, eroding the foundation of public rationality.

Sellars would remind us that a healthy society must respect both the “space of causes” and the “space of reasons,” recognizing not only causal mechanisms of social structures but also the normative significance of citizens’ rational critique.
