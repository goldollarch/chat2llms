基于朱熹核心哲学思想的陈京元“寻衅滋事罪”案分析评论

An Analysis of the Chen Jingyuan “Picking Quarrels and Provoking Trouble” Case Based on Zhu Xi’s Core Philosophical Ideas

中文版
Chinese Version

朱熹（1130-1200），宋代理学家，其核心思想以“理气论”（theory of li and qi）和“格物致知”（investigating things to extend knowledge）为中心，强调“理”为宇宙本原，外在客观存在于万物，道德修养通过“穷理尽性”内省实现天人合一，反对主观独断与权威偏私，主张“存天理，灭人欲”，言论自由为格物致知与仁义和谐的工具。本案中，陈京元博士因X平台转发艺术作品、时政观点等内容（粉丝不足百人、互动近零），被以“寻衅滋事罪”判处有期徒刑一年八个月，程序中充斥主观推定、剥夺自辩与选择性执法。从朱熹视角，此案非天理和谐，而是外在独断背离穷理格物：压制仁义表达，违背理气统一与道德修养的根本信念。

Zhu Xi (1130-1200), Song Neo-Confucian philosopher, centered his ideas on the “theory of li and qi” and “investigating things to extend knowledge” (gewu zhizhi), stressing “principle” (li) as cosmic origin, objectively external in all things, moral cultivation via “exhausting principle to perfect nature” (qiong li jin xing) introspection achieving heaven-human unity, opposing subjective fiat and authoritative bias, advocating “preserving heavenly principle, extinguishing human desires,” free speech as tool for investigating things, knowing principle, and benevolent harmony. In this case, Dr. Chen Jingyuan, an independent scholar, was sentenced to one year and eight months’ imprisonment for “picking quarrels and provoking trouble” due to forwarding artistic works and political commentary on X (formerly Twitter)—with fewer than 100 followers and near-zero engagement—amid procedural flaws like subjective presumption, denial of self-defense, and selective enforcement. From Zhu Xi’s viewpoint, this is not heavenly principle harmony but external fiat violating exhausting principle: suppressing benevolent expression, betraying li-qi unity and moral cultivation.

一、朱熹哲学核心思想概述：理气论与格物致知

I. Overview of Zhu Xi’s Core Philosophical Ideas: Theory of Li and Qi and Investigating Things to Extend Knowledge

朱熹的核心思想是“理气论”：理为客观本原，气为载道之器，道德源于“格物致知”穷理、内省修养，实现天理流行与仁义和谐，反对外在权威独断，主张“存天理，灭人欲”，言论自由为致知工具，反对主观偏私破坏理气统一。 他强调“仁义礼智信”为永恒价值，社会和谐源于内在道德觉醒。 原则：客观穷理、内省修养、和谐统一，反对独断权威与偏私执法。

Zhu Xi’s core ideas are the “theory of li and qi”: principle (li) as objective origin, qi (material force) as vehicle, morality from “investigating things to extend knowledge” exhausting principle, introspective cultivation achieving heavenly principle flux and benevolent harmony, opposing external arbitrary fiat, advocating “preserving heavenly principle, extinguishing human desires,” free speech as tool for extending knowledge, opposing subjective bias violating li-qi unity. He stressed “benevolence, righteousness, propriety, wisdom, trust” as eternal values, social harmony from internal moral awakening. Principles: objective exhausting principle, introspective cultivation, harmonious unity, opposing arbitrary authority and biased enforcement.

二、以朱熹哲学核心思想评析本案

II. Analysis of the Case Based on Zhu Xi’s Core Philosophical Ideas

  1. 外在独断背离理气论：违背客观穷理与道德觉醒原则
    朱熹视道德源于客观穷理，反对外在权威独断。 本案判决将陈京元转发的情感表达（如讽刺帖）、理性观点（如智库报告）和艺术作品（如漫画隐喻）泛化为“虚假言论”，无证据穷理证明危害，却以主观“明知”推定判“寻衅滋事”，背离理气。 账号数据显示零互动、无理气断裂，却被“梳理”为“铁证”，这正是朱熹斥的外求：司法未格物致知陈京元仁义意图（学术道德），而是外在独断，摧毁觉醒。 朱熹若在，必判此不天理——非和谐统一，乃独断暴政。

  2. External Fiat Betraying the Theory of Li and Qi: Violating Objective Exhausted Principle and Moral Awakening Principles
    Zhu Xi saw morality from objective exhausted principle, opposing external arbitrary fiat. The judgment generalizes Dr. Chen’s forwarded emotional expressions (e.g., satirical posts), rational opinions (e.g., think tank reports), and artistic works (e.g., metaphorical cartoons) as “false statements,” without objective exhausted principle evidence of harm, presuming “knowing falsehood” for “picking quarrels,” betraying li-qi. Account data shows zero engagement, no li-qi rupture, yet “collated” as “ironclad evidence”—precisely Zhu Xi’s external seeking critique: judiciary fails investigating things to extend knowledge of Dr. Chen’s benevolent intentions (academic morality), external fiat, annihilating awakening. Zhu Xi would deem this non-heavenly principle—not harmonious unity, but fiat tyranny.

  3. 权威压制扭曲格物致知：背离道德修养与和谐统一
    朱熹强调格物致知通过包容实现和谐，反对权威独断。 陈京元转发系仁义表达（如复杂系统引用），以穷理包容多元，却被禁自辩（庭审“闭嘴”）、拒转控控书，程序中“选择性执法”（党媒同类未责）压制修养，背离统一。 这违背朱熹：道德需内在觉醒与仁理，非外在规训；社会和谐需包容自由，非独断。 朱熹批判：此案非法，乃对和谐之战。

  4. Authority Suppression Twisting Investigating Things to Extend Knowledge: Betraying Moral Cultivation and Harmonious Unity
    Zhu Xi stressed investigating things to extend knowledge via inclusivity for harmony, opposing arbitrary fiat. Dr. Chen’s forwards embody benevolent expression (e.g., complex systems citations), exhausted principle inclusive of diversity, yet he was denied self-defense (courtroom “silencing”), his indictment letter rejected, and selective enforcement suppressed cultivation (state media reposts unpunished), violating unity. This violates Zhu Xi: morality requires internal awakening and benevolent principle, not external discipline; social harmony needs inclusive liberty, not fiat. Zhu Xi indicts: this is unlawful, war on harmony.

  5. 仁理觉醒失衡：荒谬警示与和谐危机
    朱熹视仁理觉醒为道德基石。 陈京元Bio自嘲“作案工具”，帖文存网无人关注，却判20月，荒谬如朱熹比喻“外求失天理”——推定“罪名”失觉醒平衡，扭曲仁理，制造危机。 这警示：失衡觉醒，永固外求。

  6. Imbalanced Benevolent Principle Awakening: Absurd Warning and Harmony Crisis
    Zhu Xi saw benevolent principle awakening as morality cornerstone. Dr. Chen’s bio mocks his account as a “tool of crime,” with posts lingering online unheeded, yet earning 20 months—absurd like Zhu Xi’s “external seeking loses heavenly principle”: presuming “crime” lacks awakening balance, twisting benevolent principle, breeding crisis. This warns: imbalanced awakening entrenches external seeking.

三、结语：重振仁理觉醒，推动和谐新生

III. Conclusion: Reviving Benevolent Principle Awakening for Harmony Rebirth

朱熹思想视本案为悲剧：独断背离穷理，压制失觉醒，永固外求危机。陈京元自辩如朱熹觉醒呼吁，重审恢复道德包容，方能让社会在仁理中新生。 如朱熹所言：“存天理，灭人欲。”唯有以此衡，方避“独断”成“公敌”，推动人文觉醒。

Zhu Xi’s ideas see this case as tragedy: fiat violates exhausted principle, suppression forfeits awakening, entrenching external seeking crisis. Dr. Chen’s defense echoes Zhu Xi’s awakening call, urging retrial to restore moral inclusivity, allowing society to rebirth in benevolent principle. As Zhu Xi stated: “Preserve heavenly principle, extinguish human desires.” Only thus can we avert “arbitrariness” as “public enemy,” advancing humanistic awakening.
