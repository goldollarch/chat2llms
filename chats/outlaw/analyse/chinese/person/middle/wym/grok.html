基于王阳明核心哲学思想的陈京元“寻衅滋事罪”案分析评论

An Analysis of the Chen Jingyuan “Picking Quarrels and Provoking Trouble” Case Based on Wang Yangming’s Core Philosophical Ideas

中文版
Chinese Version

王阳明（1472-1529），明代心学家，其核心思想以“心即理”（the mind is principle）、“知行合一”（unity of knowledge and action）和“致良知”（extending innate knowledge）为中心，强调心性本具天理，道德源于内在良知直觉，反对程朱理学之外求，通过内省修养实现“现成良知”的觉醒与社会和谐，视言论自由为良知表达与知行统一的工具，反对权威独断破坏心性统一。本案中，陈京元博士因X平台转发艺术作品、时政观点等内容（粉丝不足百人、互动近零），被以“寻衅滋事罪”判处有期徒刑一年八个月，程序中充斥主观推定、剥夺自辩与选择性执法。从王阳明视角，此案非心性觉醒，而是外在权威背离良知致用：摧毁知行合一，违背内在道德与和谐的根本信念。

Wang Yangming (1472-1529), Ming Neo-Confucian philosopher and founder of the School of Mind (xinxue), centered his ideas on “the mind is principle” (xin ji li), “unity of knowledge and action” (zhi xing he yi), and “extending innate knowledge” (zhi liang zhi), stressing that mind-nature inherently possesses heavenly principle, morality from intuitive innate knowledge, opposing Cheng-Zhu external seeking, through introspective cultivation achieving “present innate knowledge” awakening and social harmony, viewing free speech as tool for innate knowledge expression and unity of knowledge-action, opposing arbitrary authority disrupting mind-nature unity. In this case, Dr. Chen Jingyuan, an independent scholar, was sentenced to one year and eight months’ imprisonment for “picking quarrels and provoking trouble” due to forwarding artistic works and political commentary on X (formerly Twitter)—with fewer than 100 followers and near-zero engagement—amid procedural flaws like subjective presumption, denial of self-defense, and selective enforcement. From Wang Yangming’s viewpoint, this is not mind-nature awakening but external authority violating innate knowledge application: annihilating unity of knowledge-action, betraying internal morality and harmony.

一、王阳明哲学核心思想概述：心即理与知行合一

I. Overview of Wang Yangming’s Core Philosophical Ideas: The Mind Is Principle and Unity of Knowledge and Action

王阳明的核心思想是“心即理”：心性本具天理，良知即是理，反对外求，主张通过“致良知”内省觉醒道德直觉，实现“知行合一”：知是行之始，行是知之成，言论自由为良知表达与行动统一的工具，反对权威独断破坏内在和谐。 他视社会为心性统一体，个体道德修养推动集体觉醒。 原则：内在觉醒、知行统一、良知直觉，反对外求权威与空谈。

Wang Yangming’s core ideas are “the mind is principle”: mind-nature inherently possesses heavenly principle, innate knowledge is principle, opposing external seeking, advocating “extending innate knowledge” for introspective moral intuition awakening, achieving “unity of knowledge and action”: knowledge is beginning of action, action completion of knowledge, free speech as tool for innate knowledge expression and unity of knowledge-action, opposing arbitrary authority disrupting internal harmony. He viewed society as mind-nature unified body, individual moral cultivation driving collective awakening. Principles: internal awakening, unity of knowledge-action, innate knowledge intuition, opposing external authority and empty talk.

二、以王阳明哲学核心思想评析本案

II. Analysis of the Case Based on Wang Yangming’s Core Philosophical Ideas

  1. 外在独断背离心即理：违背良知直觉与内在觉醒原则
    王阳明视道德源于良知直觉，反对外在权威独断。 本案判决将陈京元转发的情感表达（如讽刺帖）、理性观点（如智库报告）和艺术作品（如漫画隐喻）泛化为“虚假言论”，无证据证明危害，却以主观“明知”推定判“寻衅滋事”，背离心性。 账号数据显示零互动、无心性冲突，却被“梳理”为“铁证”，这正是王阳明斥的外求：司法未致良知内省陈京元仁心意图（学术表达），而是外在独断，摧毁觉醒。 王阳明若在，必判此不心性——非良知觉醒，乃独断暴政。

  2. External Fiat Betraying the Mind Is Principle: Violating Innate Knowledge Intuition and Internal Awakening Principles
    Wang Yangming saw morality from innate knowledge intuition, opposing external arbitrary fiat. The judgment categorizes Dr. Chen’s forwarded emotional expressions (e.g., satirical posts), rational opinions (e.g., think tank reports), and artistic works (e.g., metaphorical cartoons) as “false statements,” without evidence of harm, presuming “knowing falsehood” for “picking quarrels,” betraying mind-nature. Account data shows zero engagement, no mind-nature conflict, yet “collated” as “ironclad evidence”—precisely Wang Yangming’s external seeking critique: judiciary fails extending innate knowledge introspecting Dr. Chen’s benevolent intentions (academic expression), external fiat, annihilating awakening. Wang Yangming would deem this non-mind-nature—not innate knowledge awakening, but fiat tyranny.

  3. 权威压制扭曲知行合一：背离道德修养与社会和谐
    王阳明强调知行合一，行动源于良知觉醒。 陈京元转发系知行统一表达（如复杂系统引用），以仁心行动促进和谐，却被禁自辩（庭审“闭嘴”）、拒转控控书，程序中“选择性执法”（党媒同类未责）压制修养，背离统一。 这违背王阳明：道德需良知行动，非外在规训；社会和谐需心性自由，非独断。 王阳明批判：此案非法，乃对合一之战。

  4. Authority Suppression Twisting Unity of Knowledge-Action: Betraying Moral Cultivation and Social Harmony
    Wang Yangming stressed unity of knowledge-action, action from innate knowledge awakening. Dr. Chen’s forwards embody unity of knowledge-action expression (e.g., complex systems citations), benevolent action promoting harmony, yet he was denied self-defense (courtroom “silencing”), his indictment letter rejected, and selective enforcement suppressed cultivation (state media reposts unpunished), violating unity. This violates Wang Yangming: morality requires innate knowledge action, not external discipline; social harmony needs mind-nature liberty, not fiat. Wang Yangming indicts: this is unlawful, war on unity.

  5. 良知觉醒失衡：荒谬警示与心性危机
    王阳明视良知觉醒为道德基石。 陈京元Bio自嘲“作案工具”，帖文存网无人关注，却判20月，荒谬如王阳明比喻“外求失天理”——推定“罪名”失觉醒平衡，扭曲良知，制造危机。 这警示：失衡觉醒，永固外求。

  6. Imbalanced Innate Knowledge Awakening: Absurd Warning and Mind-Nature Crisis
    Wang Yangming saw innate knowledge awakening as morality cornerstone. Dr. Chen’s bio mocks his account as a “tool of crime,” with posts lingering online unheeded, yet earning 20 months—absurd like Wang Yangming’s “external seeking loses heavenly principle”: presuming “crime” lacks awakening balance, twisting innate knowledge, breeding crisis. This warns: imbalanced awakening entrenches external seeking.

三、结语：重振心性觉醒，推动和谐新生

III. Conclusion: Reviving Mind-Nature Awakening for Harmony Rebirth

王阳明思想视本案为悲剧：独断背离心理，压制失合一，永固外求危机。陈京元自辩如王阳明良知呼吁，重审恢复道德直觉，方能让社会在觉醒中新生。 如王阳明所言：“心即理。”唯有以此衡，方避“独断”成“公敌”，推动人文觉醒。

Wang Yangming’s ideas see this case as tragedy: fiat betrays mind-principle, suppression forfeits unity, entrenching external seeking crisis. Dr. Chen’s defense echoes Wang Yangming’s innate knowledge call, urging retrial to restore moral intuition, allowing society to rebirth in awakening. As Wang Yangming stated: “The mind is principle.” Only thus can we avert “arbitrariness” as “public enemy,” advancing humanistic awakening.
