基于詹巴蒂斯塔·维科（Giambattista Vico）历史与哲学核心思想的陈京元“寻衅滋事罪”案分析评论

An Analysis of the Chen Jingyuan “Picking Quarrels and Provoking Trouble” Case Based on Giambattista Vico’s Core Ideas in History and Philosophy

中文版
Chinese Version

詹巴蒂斯塔·维科（Giambattista Vico，1668-1744），意大利历史哲学家，其核心思想以《新科学》（The New Science，1725）为中心，提出“历史循环论”（cyclical theory of history）：文明发展通过三个阶段——神时代（age of gods）、英雄时代（age of heroes）、人类时代（age of men），然后衰落循环，反对笛卡尔理性主义，强调“verum factum principle”（我们能理解我们自己创造的东西），历史是人类集体想象力（collective imagination）的产物，通过神话、语言与习俗揭示真理。 他视历史为有机过程，权威独断加速循环衰落。 本案中，陈京元博士因X平台转发艺术作品、时政观点等内容（粉丝不足百人、互动近零），被以“寻衅滋事罪”判处有期徒刑一年八个月，程序中充斥主观推定、剥夺自辩与选择性执法。从维科视角，此案非历史真理揭示，而是权威独断背离集体想象：摧毁人类时代表达，违背循环觉醒的根本信念。

Giambattista Vico (1668-1744), Italian philosopher of history, centered his ideas on The New Science (1725), proposing a “cyclical theory of history”: civilizations develop through three stages—the age of gods, age of heroes, age of men—then decline in cycles, opposing Cartesian rationalism, stressing the “verum factum principle” (we understand what we ourselves make), history as product of collective imagination via myth, language, and custom revealing truth. He viewed history as organic process, arbitrary authority accelerating cyclical decline. In this case, Dr. Chen Jingyuan, an independent scholar, was sentenced to one year and eight months’ imprisonment for “picking quarrels and provoking trouble” due to forwarding artistic works and political commentary on X (formerly Twitter)—with fewer than 100 followers and near-zero engagement—amid procedural flaws like subjective presumption, denial of self-defense, and selective enforcement. From Vico’s viewpoint, this is not historical truth-revelation but arbitrary authority violating collective imagination: annihilating human-age expression, betraying cyclical awakening.

一、维科历史哲学核心思想概述：历史循环论与verum factum原则

I. Overview of Vico’s Core Ideas in Historical Philosophy: Cyclical Theory of History and the Verum Factum Principle

维科的核心思想是“历史循环论”：文明如有机体循环，神时代（神话权威主导）、英雄时代（贵族英雄主义）、人类时代（理性民主），然后衰落为混乱，再循环；反对线性进步，强调人类集体创造历史，真理源于“verum factum”（我们创造故我们理解）。 他视语言、神话与习俗为历史灵魂，反对抽象理性独断。 原则：历史是想象力过程，反对权威僵化，推动集体觉醒与循环超越。

Vico’s core ideas are the “cyclical theory of history”: civilizations cycle organically—age of gods (mythic authority), age of heroes (aristocratic heroism), age of men (rational democracy)—then decline to chaos, recycling; opposing linear progress, stressing human collective creation of history, truth from “verum factum” (we make, hence we understand). He viewed language, myth, and custom as history’s soul, opposing abstract rational fiat. Principles: history as imaginative process, opposing authority rigidity, promoting collective awakening and cyclical transcendence.

二、以维科历史哲学核心思想评析本案

II. Analysis of the Case Based on Vico’s Core Ideas in Historical Philosophy

  1. 权威独断加速循环衰落：违背人类时代与集体想象原则
    维科视人类时代为理性集体想象主导，反对权威神话回归。 本案判决将陈京元转发的情感表达（如讽刺帖）、理性观点（如智库报告）与艺术作品（如漫画隐喻）泛化为“虚假言论”，无证据证明危害，却以主观“明知”推定判“寻衅滋事”，体现神话权威独断。 账号数据显示零互动、无历史涟漪，却被“梳理”为“铁证”，这正是维科斥的衰落：司法神话化“秩序”，抹杀集体想象（学者表达），加速从人类时代向混乱循环。 维科若在，必判此不历史——非想象觉醒，乃神话暴政。

  2. Arbitrary Authority Accelerating Cyclical Decline: Violating the Human Age and Collective Imagination Principles
    Vico saw the human age as rational collective imagination dominant, opposing mythic authority regression. The judgment categorizes Dr. Chen’s forwarded emotional expressions (e.g., satirical posts), rational opinions (e.g., think tank reports), and artistic works (e.g., metaphorical cartoons) as “false statements,” without evidence of harm, presuming “knowing falsehood” for “picking quarrels,” embodying mythic authority fiat. Account data shows zero engagement, no rippling, yet “collated” as “ironclad evidence”—precisely Vico’s decline critique: judiciary mythologizes “order,” erasing collective imagination (scholarly expression), accelerating from human age to chaotic cycle. Vico would deem this non-historical—not imaginative awakening, but mythic tyranny.

  3. 神话权威压制与历史有机失衡：背离verum factum与语言习俗
    维科强调历史通过语言与习俗集体创造，反对独断神话。 陈京元转发系有机表达（如复杂系统引用），源于人类习俗辩论，却被禁自辩（庭审“闭嘴”）、拒转控控书，程序中“选择性执法”（党媒同类未责）神话权威，背离verum factum。 这违背维科：历史须集体理解创造，非权威规训；语言自由非独断。 维科批判：此案非法，乃对有机之战。

  4. Mythic Authority Suppression and Imbalanced Historical Organic: Betraying Verum Factum and Language/Custom
    Vico stressed history collectively created through language and custom, opposing fiat myth. Dr. Chen’s forwards represent organic expression (e.g., complex systems citations), from human custom debate, yet he was denied self-defense (courtroom “silencing”), his indictment letter rejected, and selective enforcement mythologized authority (state media reposts unpunished), violating verum factum. This violates Vico: history requires collective creative understanding, not authoritative discipline; language liberty, not fiat. Vico indicts: this is unlawful, war on the organic.

  5. 集体想象失衡：荒谬警示与循环危机
    维科视集体想象为历史灵魂。 陈京元Bio自嘲“作案工具”，帖文存网无人关注，却判20月，荒谬如维科比喻“神话遮蔽人类”——推定“罪名”失想象平衡，扭曲循环，制造危机。 这警示：失衡想象，永固衰落。

  6. Imbalanced Collective Imagination: Absurd Warning and Cyclical Crisis
    Vico saw collective imagination as history’s soul. Dr. Chen’s bio mocks his account as a “tool of crime,” with posts lingering online unheeded, yet earning 20 months—absurd like Vico’s “myth obscuring human”: presuming “crime” lacks imaginative balance, twisting cycles, breeding crisis. This warns: imbalanced imagination entrenches decline.

三、结语：重振集体创造，推动循环新生

III. Conclusion: Reviving Collective Creation for Cyclical Rebirth

维科思想视本案为悲剧：独断加速衰落，压制失想象，永固循环危机。陈京元自辩如维科觉醒呼吁，重审恢复语言自由，方能让历史在集体中新生。 如维科所言：“我们理解我们创造的。”唯有以此衡，方避“神话”成“公敌”，推动人文循环。

Vico’s ideas see this case as tragedy: fiat accelerates decline, suppression forfeits imagination, entrenching cyclical crisis. Dr. Chen’s defense echoes Vico’s awakening call, urging retrial to restore language liberty, allowing history to rebirth in collective. As Vico stated: “We understand what we make.” Only thus can we avert “myth” as “public enemy,” advancing humanistic cycles.
