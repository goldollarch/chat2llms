以下是基于《中华人民共和国宪法》有关公民基本权利的相关规定，对陈京元案进行的分析与评论，并提供中英文对照版本。

宪法保障与权利受限：《中华人民共和国宪法》视角下的陈京元案

Constitutional Guarantees and Restricted Rights: A Perspective from the Constitution of the People’s Republic of China on the Chen Jingyuan Case

一、宪法核心规定概述

I. Core Provisions of the Constitution

  • 中文：《中华人民共和国宪法》明确规定公民享有以下基本权利：

    • 言论、出版、集会、结社、游行、示威自由（第35条）。

    • 人身自由不受侵犯（第37条）。

    • 人格尊严不受侵犯（第38条）。

    • 监督权与批评权（第41条）：公民有权对国家机关和工作人员提出批评、建议和申诉。

  • English: The Constitution of the People’s Republic of China explicitly guarantees citizens the following fundamental rights:

    • Freedom of speech, press, assembly, association, procession, and demonstration (Article 35).

    • Personal freedom shall not be violated (Article 37).

    • Human dignity shall not be infringed (Article 38).

    • Right to supervision and criticism (Article 41): Citizens have the right to criticize, make suggestions, and lodge complaints against state organs and officials.

二、案件分析

II. Case Analysis

1. 模糊罪名与言论自由的冲突

  • 中文：以“寻衅滋事”定罪，罪名模糊，直接限制了公民的言论自由，违背宪法第35条的保障。

  • English: Conviction under “picking quarrels,” with vague charges, directly restricts freedom of speech, contradicting Article 35’s guarantee.

2. 人格尊严与监督权的否定

  • 中文：陈京元之言源于公共关怀，属于公民监督与批评权的体现。制度若压制此类表达，等于否定宪法第41条所保障的监督权，并损害公民人格尊严。

  • English: Chen’s speech stems from public concern, reflecting citizens’ right to supervision and criticism. Suppressing such expression denies Article 41’s protection and undermines human dignity.

3. 法治精神与宪法原则的背离

  • 中文：宪法要求“依法治国”，法律必须明确、公正、保障权利。模糊法条的适用，背离了宪法确立的法治精神。

  • English: The Constitution requires “governing the country according to law,” with laws being clear, just, and rights-protective. Applying vague statutes deviates from the constitutional spirit of rule of law.

三、结语与启示

III. Conclusion and Reflections

  • 中文：《中华人民共和国宪法》提醒我们，制度的合法性在于保障公民基本权利。陈京元案所显之压制，是对宪法保障的背离，也是对法治根基的动摇。

  • English: The Constitution of the PRC reminds us that institutional legitimacy lies in safeguarding citizens’ fundamental rights. The suppression in Chen’s case departs from constitutional guarantees and undermines the foundation of rule of law.

  • 中文：若制度不能落实宪法所保障的权利，社会将失去公民信任，法治也将失去正当性。

  • English: If institutions fail to implement constitutional rights, society loses citizens’ trust, and the rule of law loses legitimacy.

参考资料 / References

  • Articles 35, 37, 38, 41 of the Constitution
