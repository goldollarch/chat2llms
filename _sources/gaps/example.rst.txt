Some specific examples
=======================

In this section, we will further explore the shortcomings and limitations of contemporary large language models. 

We will begin by presenting a few examples of meta-analyses — specifically, analyses conducted by major large language models (such as Grok, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Qwen) regarding other large language models. 

Through these examples, we aim to identify the weaknesses and deficiencies of each model, thereby gaining insights into the direction and pathways for the future development of large language models.
