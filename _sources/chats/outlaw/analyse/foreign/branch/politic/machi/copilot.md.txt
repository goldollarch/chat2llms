---

## 一、权力的现实主义

### Realism of Power

**中文**：
马基雅维里强调，政治的核心是真实的权力运作，而非理想化的道德说辞。在陈京元案中，国家机关以“寻衅滋事”为名惩罚思想表达，表面上是维护公共秩序，实质上是通过法律与暴力机器展示权力的威慑力。马基雅维里会认为，这种做法体现了统治者“维持权力”的首要目标，而非真正的法律正义。

**English**:
Machiavelli emphasized that the essence of politics lies in the realistic exercise of power, not in idealized moral rhetoric. In Chen Jingyuan’s case, the state punished expression under the charge of “picking quarrels,” ostensibly to maintain public order but in reality to demonstrate the deterrent force of law and coercion. Machiavelli would see this as reflecting the ruler’s primary goal of preserving power rather than upholding genuine justice.

---

## 二、目的与手段的关系

### The Relationship Between Ends and Means

**中文**：
马基雅维里著名地指出，君主在必要时必须“不择手段”以维持国家的稳定与权威。在本案中，国家通过模糊罪名、选择性执法来打击异议，这正是“手段”服务于“目的”的体现。虽然这种做法可能在短期内巩固了统治，但从长远看，它削弱了法律的公信力与社会的信任。

**English**:
Machiavelli famously argued that rulers must, when necessary, use any means to preserve the stability and authority of the state. In this case, the state employed vague charges and selective enforcement to suppress dissent, a clear example of means serving ends. While such tactics may consolidate rule in the short term, in the long run they erode the credibility of law and the trust of society.

---

## 三、恐惧与秩序的政治逻辑

### The Political Logic of Fear and Order

**中文**：
马基雅维里认为，君主最好是“既被爱又被怕”，但如果不能兼得，“被怕”比“被爱”更安全。本案中，国家通过高调的抓捕与定罪，制造恐惧氛围，以此来震慑潜在的异议者。这种逻辑正是马基雅维里式的：通过恐惧来维持秩序。然而，他也提醒过，过度依赖恐惧可能导致民众的怨恨与反抗。

**English**:
Machiavelli argued that it is best for a ruler to be both loved and feared, but if one cannot have both, it is safer to be feared. In this case, the state’s dramatic arrest and conviction created an atmosphere of fear, deterring potential dissenters. This logic is distinctly Machiavellian: maintaining order through fear. Yet he also warned that excessive reliance on fear risks breeding resentment and resistance among the people.

---

## 四、表象与实质

### Appearance vs. Substance

**中文**：
马基雅维里强调，君主不仅要掌握权力，还要善于制造“正义与合法”的表象。本案中，法院通过程序化的审理与判决，营造出“依法办案”的形象，掩盖了其政治性动机。这正是马基雅维里所说的：统治者必须看似遵循法律与道德，即使其实质是出于权力考量。

**English**:
Machiavelli stressed that rulers must not only wield power but also cultivate the appearance of justice and legality. In this case, the court’s procedural trial and verdict created the image of “lawful adjudication,” masking its political motives. This is precisely what Machiavelli described: rulers must appear to follow law and morality, even when their substance is driven by power considerations.

---

## 五、结论

### Conclusion

**中文**：从马基雅维里的视角看，陈京元案是典型的“权力政治”案例：

- 国家以法律为手段，实质上维护的是统治权力；
- 恐惧被用来维持秩序，但长期可能引发反作用；
- 法律程序的表象掩盖了政治动机。

马基雅维里会提醒我们：理解此类案件，必须超越法律文本，看到其背后的权力逻辑。

**English**:From Machiavelli’s perspective, Chen Jingyuan’s case is a classic example of “politics of power”:

- The state used law as a means to preserve authority.
- Fear was employed to maintain order, though it may backfire in the long run.
- Legal procedures masked political motives.

Machiavelli would remind us that to understand such cases, one must look beyond legal texts to the underlying logic of power.
