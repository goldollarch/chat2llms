English: From Denis Diderot’s core philosophy, the Chen Jingyuan case reflects the danger of law being used to suppress thought and expression, contradicting Diderot’s advocacy of rational critique, intellectual freedom, and the spirit of public enlightenment.

### 一、狄德罗哲学核心思想  
**Core Ideas of Diderot’s Philosophy**

- 中文：狄德罗是法国启蒙运动的代表人物，主编《百科全书》，强调**知识的普及与共享**，反对封建专制与思想垄断。  
- English: Diderot, a leading figure of the French Enlightenment and editor of the *Encyclopédie*, emphasized **the dissemination and sharing of knowledge**, opposing feudal despotism and monopolies of thought.  

- 中文：他坚持**唯物主义与经验论**，认为真理应通过观察、实验与理性批判获得，而非依赖权威。  
- English: He upheld **materialism and empiricism**, arguing that truth must be grounded in observation, experiment, and rational critique rather than authority.  

- 中文：狄德罗倡导**思想自由与宽容**，认为社会进步依赖于多元声音的交流与辩论。  
- English: Diderot promoted **freedom of thought and tolerance**, believing that social progress depends on the exchange and debate of diverse voices.  

---

### 二、案件分析  
**Case Analysis**

#### 1. 知识与言论的自由 / Freedom of Knowledge and Expression  
- 中文：陈京元因转发公共信息而获罪，正是狄德罗所反对的“思想禁锢”。狄德罗认为，知识应当向公众开放，而非因表达而受惩罚。  
- English: Dr. Chen’s conviction for reposting public information exemplifies the “imprisonment of thought” Diderot opposed. For him, knowledge should be open to the public, not punished for expression.  

#### 2. 理性批判的缺失 / Absence of Rational Critique  
- 中文：判决以推定代替证据，缺乏经验与理性论证。这与狄德罗强调的“以理性和实证为基础的真理”背道而驰。  
- English: The judgment relied on presumption rather than evidence, lacking empirical and rational justification. This contradicts Diderot’s insistence that truth must rest on reason and empiricism.  

#### 3. 公共启蒙的阻碍 / Obstruction of Public Enlightenment  
- 中文：狄德罗主张通过知识传播促进社会启蒙。本案通过刑罚压制学者的表达，制造寒蝉效应，阻碍了公共理性的发展。  
- English: Diderot saw knowledge dissemination as key to enlightenment. By punishing a scholar’s expression, this case chills discourse and obstructs the growth of public reason.  

#### 4. 法律的工具化 / Instrumentalization of Law  
- 中文：狄德罗批判专制政体利用法律压制思想。本案以模糊罪名“寻衅滋事”定罪，正是法律被工具化的表现。  
- English: Diderot criticized despotic regimes for using law to suppress thought. Convicting under the vague charge of “picking quarrels” exemplifies this instrumentalization of law.  

---

### 三、结论与启示  
**Conclusion and Reflections**

- 中文：从狄德罗的哲学出发，陈京元案不仅是个体的不幸，更是社会启蒙受阻的象征。真正的法治应当保障知识自由、鼓励理性批判，并以法律促进公共启蒙，而非成为压制思想的工具。  
- English: From Diderot’s philosophy, the Chen Jingyuan case is not only an individual tragedy but also a symbol of obstructed enlightenment. Genuine rule of law must safeguard intellectual freedom, encourage rational critique, and use law to promote public enlightenment—not to suppress thought.
