**Adam Smith’s Core Ideas**

- 中文：《道德情操论》中，斯密强调**同情（sympathy）**是道德的根源，人类通过“公正的旁观者”来判断行为的合宜性。  
- English: In *The Theory of Moral Sentiments*, Smith emphasized **sympathy** as the root of morality, with the “impartial spectator” serving as the standard for judging propriety.  

- 中文：《国富论》中，他提出“看不见的手”，强调个体自由与社会秩序之间的自发协调机制。  
- English: In *The Wealth of Nations*, he introduced the “invisible hand,” highlighting the spontaneous coordination between individual liberty and social order.
