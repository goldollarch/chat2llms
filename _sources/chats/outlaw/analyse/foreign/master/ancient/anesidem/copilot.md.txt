*Aenesidemus’ Skeptical Perspective on the Case of Chen Jingyuan*

## 一、怀疑主义的基本立场  
**中文**：埃奈西德穆继承并发展了皮浪怀疑主义，强调人类认知的有限性与相对性。他提出的“十种怀疑方式”旨在表明：任何断言都可以从不同角度被质疑，因此我们应当悬置判断（epoché），避免武断地宣称“真”或“假”。  

**English**: Aenesidemus, reviving Pyrrhonian skepticism, emphasized the limits and relativity of human cognition. His “Ten Modes of Skepticism” demonstrate that any assertion can be challenged from multiple perspectives, leading to the suspension of judgment (*epoché*) rather than dogmatic claims of truth or falsehood.  

---

## 二、案件中的“虚假信息”问题  
**中文**：法院认定被告“转发虚假信息”，但并未逐条论证何为“虚假”。怀疑主义提醒我们：  
- **感知差异**（第一方式）：同一信息在不同人眼中可能呈现不同意义。  
- **文化与立场差异**（第二方式）：在官方语境下被视为“虚假”，在学术或国际语境下可能被视为“观点”或“批评”。  
- **相对性**（第十方式）：所谓“虚假”与“真实”往往依赖于参照系，而非绝对。  

**English**: The court labeled the reposted content as “false information” without substantiating why. Skepticism reminds us:  
- **Differences in perception** (Mode 1): The same message may appear differently to different observers.  
- **Cultural and contextual relativity** (Mode 2): What is “false” in an official context may be “opinion” or “critique” in academic or international discourse.  
- **Relativity** (Mode 10): “Truth” and “falsehood” are often dependent on frameworks of reference, not absolutes.  

---

## 三、关于“明知”的推定  
**中文**：判决书以“学历高，应能辨别是非”推定被告“明知虚假”。怀疑主义会指出：  
- **认知局限**：即使高学历者，也无法超越人类普遍的知识有限性。  
- **怀疑之德**：真正的学者往往保持开放与批判，而非断然认定真假。  
- 因此，以学历推定“明知”，本身就是一种**教条化的逻辑跳跃**。  

**English**: The judgment assumed that “with high education, he must have known it was false.” Skepticism would argue:  
- **Limits of cognition**: Even the highly educated cannot transcend the universal limits of human knowledge.  
- **Virtue of doubt**: Scholars often maintain openness and critical distance rather than dogmatic certainty.  
- Thus, inferring “knowledge of falsity” from education is itself a **dogmatic leap**.  

---

## 四、关于“扰乱公共秩序”的认定  
**中文**：怀疑主义要求我们检视证据是否真的显示“严重混乱”。  
- **现象与解释的差异**：少量转发的事实，与“严重混乱”的解释之间存在巨大落差。  
- **因果关系的不可确证**：怀疑主义提醒我们，因果链条往往难以确立，尤其在社会传播中。  

**English**: Skepticism asks us to examine whether evidence truly shows “serious disorder.”  
- **Phenomenon vs. interpretation**: The fact of a few reposts is far from the interpretation of “serious disruption.”  
- **Uncertainty of causality**: Skepticism highlights the difficulty of proving causal chains, especially in social communication.  

---

## 五、怀疑主义的启示  
**中文**：  
- 埃奈西德穆会提醒我们：在真假未明、因果未证的情况下，最合理的态度是**悬置判断**，而不是草率定罪。  
- 法院的做法恰恰相反：它以怀疑主义本应避免的方式，将不确定性转化为确定性，并以此剥夺公民自由。  

**English**:  
- Aenesidemus would remind us: when truth is unclear and causality unproven, the most reasonable stance is **suspension of judgment**, not hasty conviction.  
- The court did the opposite: it transformed uncertainty into certainty, precisely what skepticism warns against, and used it to justify depriving a citizen of liberty.  

---

## 六、总结  
**中文**：从怀疑主义的角度看，本案的最大问题在于：它没有承认认知与判断的相对性与不确定性，而是将“怀疑”转化为“定罪”。这不仅违背了罪刑法定与无罪推定原则，也违背了哲学上对真理的谦卑态度。  

**English**: From a skeptical perspective, the core flaw of this case lies in its refusal to acknowledge the relativity and uncertainty of judgment. It transformed “doubt” into “conviction,” violating not only the legal principles of legality and presumption of innocence but also the philosophical humility toward truth.
