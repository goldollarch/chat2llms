### 基于周敦颐核心哲学思想的陈京元“寻衅滋事罪”案分析评论  
### An Analysis of the Chen Jingyuan "Picking Quarrels and Provoking Trouble" Case Based on Zhou Dunyi's Core Philosophical Ideas

**中文版**  
**Chinese Version**

周敦颐（1017-1073），宋代理学先驱，其核心思想以《太极图说》和《通书》为中心，强调“无极而太极”（from wuji to taiji）和“主静”（presiding stillness），视宇宙生成于太极生两仪、四象、八卦的动态平衡，道德修养通过静观仁心实现天人合一，反对外在权威独断，主张“仁为本，静为基”，言论自由为仁心表达与阴阳和谐的工具。本案中，陈京元博士因X平台转发艺术作品、时政观点等内容（粉丝不足百人、互动近零），被以“寻衅滋事罪”判处有期徒刑一年八个月，程序中充斥主观推定、剥夺自辩与选择性执法。从周敦颐视角，此案非太极和谐，而是外在独断背离主静仁心：压制仁心表达，违背无极生太极的根本信念。

Zhou Dunyi (1017-1073), pioneer of Song Neo-Confucianism, centered his ideas on *Diagram of the Supreme Ultimate Explained* (Taiji Tu Shuo) and *Penetrating Book* (Tong Shu), stressing "from wuji to taiji" and "presiding stillness" (zhu jing), viewing cosmic generation as taiji birthing two polarities, four symbols, eight trigrams in dynamic balance, moral cultivation via contemplative benevolence achieving heaven-human unity, opposing external arbitrary fiat, advocating "benevolence as root, stillness as base," free speech as tool for benevolent expression and yin-yang harmony. In this case, Dr. Chen Jingyuan, an independent scholar, was sentenced to one year and eight months' imprisonment for "picking quarrels and provoking trouble" due to forwarding artistic works and political commentary on X (formerly Twitter)—with fewer than 100 followers and near-zero engagement—amid procedural flaws like subjective presumption, denial of self-defense, and selective enforcement. From Zhou Dunyi's viewpoint, this is not taiji harmony but external fiat violating contemplative benevolence: suppressing benevolent expression, betraying wuji to taiji.

#### 一、周敦颐哲学核心思想概述：无极而太极与主静仁心  
#### I. Overview of Zhou Dunyi's Core Philosophical Ideas: From Wuji to Taiji and Presiding Stillness in Benevolence  

周敦颐的核心思想是“无极而太极”：宇宙从无极生太极，太极动静生阴阳五行，道德修养通过“主静”内观仁心实现天人合一，反对外在权威独断，主张静中养道，仁为主宰，言论自由为仁心觉醒与阴阳平衡的表达。 他强调“仁为本，静为基”，社会和谐源于内在静观而非强制统一。 原则：动态平衡、内观觉醒、仁心包容，反对外求权威与独断。

Zhou Dunyi's core ideas are "from wuji to taiji": universe from wuji births taiji, taiji's motion-stillness births yin-yang five elements, moral cultivation via "presiding stillness" internal contemplation of benevolence achieving heaven-human unity, opposing external arbitrary fiat, advocating nurturing the Way in stillness, benevolence as master, free speech as expression of benevolent awakening and yin-yang balance. He stressed "benevolence as root, stillness as base," social harmony from internal contemplation not coercive unity. Principles: dynamic balance, internal awakening, benevolent inclusivity, opposing external authority and fiat.

#### II. Analysis of the Case Based on Zhou Dunyi's Core Philosophical Ideas

Zhou Dunyi would deem this non-benevolent—not awakening harmony, but fiat tyranny.

2. **权威压制扭曲主静仁心：背离内观修养与天人合一**  
   周敦颐强调主静内观仁心实现和谐，反对外在独断。 陈京元转发系仁心表达（如复杂系统引用），以静观包容多元，却被禁自辩（庭审“闭嘴”）、拒转控控书，程序中“选择性执法”（党媒同类未责）压制修养，背离合一。 这违背周敦颐：道德需仁心觉醒，非外在规训；社会和谐需静观自由，非独断。 周敦颐批判：此案非法，乃对仁心之战。

   Zhou Dunyi stressed presiding stillness internal contemplation of benevolence for harmony, opposing external arbitrary fiat. Dr. Chen's forwards embody benevolent expression (e.g., complex systems citations), contemplative benevolence inclusive of diversity, yet he was denied self-defense (courtroom "silencing"), his indictment letter rejected, and selective enforcement suppressed cultivation (state media reposts unpunished), violating unity.
