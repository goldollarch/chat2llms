Meta-Analyses

In this section, we will further explore the shortcomings and limitations of contemporary large language models.

We will begin by presenting a few examples of meta-analyses — specifically, analyses conducted by major large language models (such as Grok, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Qwen) regarding analyses conducted by other large language models.

Through these examples, we aim to identify the weaknesses and deficiencies of various large language models, in order to gain insights into the direction and pathways for their further development.
