敦促普会峻等认罪伏法书
----------------------
An Exhortation for Pu Huijun and Others to Confess Their Crimes and Submit to the Law

-----------------------------------------------

普会峻、李湘云、葛斌诸君鉴：

To Pu Huijun, Li Xiangyun, Ge Bin, and others:

昆明城下，秋风萧瑟。尔等身为司法公仆，本应秉持国徽，守护宪法尊严，维护公平正义。今却在陈京元博士一案中，枉顾法理，肆意妄为，酿成惊天冤案。全面依法治国大势已成，党中央和国家政府反复强调从严治党、依宪治国、司法公正，最高人民法院2025年工作报告更明令限缩“口袋罪”适用，防范冤假错案。尔等逆潮流而动，执迷不悟，必将陷于绝境。尔等尚知悔悟否？今奉劝尔等悬崖勒马，速速认罪伏法，争取宽大处理。

Beneath the city of Kunming, the autumn winds blow bleakly. You, as public servants in the judiciary, should uphold the national emblem, safeguard the dignity of the Constitution, and maintain fairness and justice. Yet, in the case of Dr. Chen Jingyuan, you have disregarded legal principles, acted arbitrarily, and created a shocking miscarriage of justice. The grand momentum of comprehensively governing the country according to law has already taken shape. The Central Committee of the Party and the state government have repeatedly emphasized strict governance of the Party, governing according to the Constitution, and judicial fairness. The 2025 Work Report of the Supreme People's Court explicitly mandates the restriction of the application of "pocket crimes" and the prevention of wrongful convictions. You have gone against the tide, remaining obstinately unrepentant, and will surely lead to a desperate situation. Do you still know how to repent? We hereby advise you to rein in at the precipice, promptly confess your crimes and submit to the law, and strive for lenient treatment.

尔等身为法官、检察官、和警官，本应以事实为依据，以法律为准绳。今却在陈京元博士一案中，公然造谣诽谤，枉法仲裁，将一介独立学者之学术探究，诬蔑为“寻衅滋事”。尔等谬论陈京元博士“高学历”应“明知”故“有罪”震古烁今，全无视其账号粉丝寥寥、转发总量不足百，转发内容多为艺术、主观情感、理论文章及历史资料，无一引发社会影响。尔等将艺术漫画如“撑伞女孩”诬为“谣言”，岂不知中华文明本以想象创造为魂？盘古开天、夸父逐日、李白“白发三千丈”，若依尔等逻辑，皆成“虚假信息”，尔等岂非欲灭绝我民族文艺之瑰宝？尔等将主观情感表达如六四烛光图片、许章润释放消息，强定为“扰乱秩序”，岂不知人类情感本为主观之体验？若依尔等荒谬，弗洛伊德所疑之梦中杀王自当死罪，尔等岂非视亿万民众为必然之“罪犯”？尔等将理论文章如政治光谱分类、《客观评价习近平》、特朗普批判共产主义演讲、蓬佩奥中美关系讲话，肆意“梳理”为“攻击侮辱”，岂不知思想争鸣乃文明之精华？波普尔批判马克思主义、哈耶克争锋凯恩斯，皆入教科书，若依尔等谬论，古今中外学者尽成“谣言散布者”！尔等将历史资料如毛泽东选集编辑修订、离休干部工资单、《我赞成小平同志退休》、中乌军事合作，妄称“恶意炮制”，岂不知这些皆可查证之事实？尔等无辟谣声明，却倒打一耙，贼喊捉贼，岂非玩忽职守、尸位素餐？

You, as Judges, prosecutors, and police officers, should base yourselves on facts and take the law as the criterion. Yet, in the case of Dr. Chen Jingyuan, you have openly engaged in slander and defamation, perverting the law in arbitration, slandering the academic inquiry of an independent scholar as "picking quarrels and provoking trouble." Your absurd claim that Dr. Chen Jingyuan's "high education" means he "should know" and thus is "guilty" is unprecedented, completely ignoring that his account has few followers, with reposts totaling less than a hundred. The reposted content mostly consists of art, subjective emotions, theoretical articles, and historical materials, none of which has caused any social impact. You slander artistic cartoons like the "Umbrella Girl" as "rumors," unaware that Chinese civilization is rooted in imagination and creation? Pangu opening the heavens, Kuafu chasing the sun, Li Bai's "white hair three thousand zhang"—according to your logic, all become "false information." Do you intend to exterminate the treasures of our nation's literature and art? You force subjective emotional expressions like the June Fourth candlelight pictures and the news of Xu Zhangrun's release to be labeled as "disrupting order," unaware that human emotions are inherently subjective experiences? According to your absurdity, Freud's dream of killing the king should also warrant the death penalty—do you regard billions of people as inevitable "criminals"? You arbitrarily "sort out" theoretical articles like political spectrum classifications, "Objective Evaluation of Xi Jinping," Trump's speech criticizing communism, and Pompeo's remarks on U.S.-China relations as "insults and attacks," unaware that intellectual debates are the essence of civilization? Popper's critique of Marxism, Hayek's confrontation with Keynes—all entered textbooks. According to your fallacies, scholars ancient and modern, Chinese and foreign, all become "rumor spreaders"! You falsely claim historical materials like the editing and revision history of Mao Zedong's Selected Works, retired cadre pay slips, "I Support Comrade Xiaoping's Retirement," and Sino-Ukrainian military cooperation as "malicious fabrications," unaware that these are verifiable facts? You have no debunking statements, yet turn around and strike back, crying thief while being the thief yourself—aren't you neglecting your duties and occupying positions without merit?

尔等造谣诽谤，虚构“谣言铁证”，抹黑陈京元博士为“寻衅滋事”之“流氓”者也，实乃对一公民人格和名誉之恶意中伤。尔等枉法仲裁，无鉴定、无数据、无因果，强塞“口袋罪”，践踏罪刑法定、无罪推定、证据裁判原则，实乃司法暴政之典型。尔等身为公仆，纳税人供养，本应守护法治，今却以权力为私器，凌驾宪法，背离全面依法治国。党中央和国家政府一再强调宽严相济、防范冤假错案，尔等顽抗到底，唯有一途：天网恢恢，疏而不漏，终身追责，至死无休！

You engage in slander and defamation, fabricating "ironclad evidence of rumors," smearing Dr. Chen Jingyuan as a "troublemaker" in "picking quarrels and provoking trouble," which is in fact a malicious injury to a citizen's personality and reputation. You pervert the law in arbitration, with no identification, no data, no causation, forcibly stuffing into the "pocket crime," trampling on the principles of legality of crimes and punishments, presumption of innocence, and evidence-based adjudication, which is a typical example of judicial tyranny. You, as public servants supported by taxpayers, should safeguard the rule of law, but now you use power as a private tool, overriding the Constitution, deviating from comprehensively governing the country according to law. The Central Committee of the Party and the state government repeatedly emphasize combining leniency with severity and preventing wrongful convictions. You stubbornly resist to the end, with only one path left: The net of heaven is vast and wide, sparse yet letting nothing slip through—lifelong accountability, pursued to death!

尔等今尚有悔悟之路：速速自首，坦白罪行，承认造谣诽谤、枉法仲裁之过。党中央和国家政府宽大政策，历来从优待悔悟者。尔等若迷途知返，可获从轻处置；若执迷不悟，顽抗到底，必将自食恶果，遗臭万年。全面依法治国，大势所趋，尔等岂能螳臂挡车、蚍蜉撼树？尔等熟思之，慎择之。

You still have a path to repentance today: promptly surrender yourselves, confess your crimes frankly, and admit the faults of slander and defamation, perverting the law in arbitration. The lenient policy of the Central Committee of the Party and the state government has always treated those who repent favorably. If you turn back from the wrong path, you can receive lenient treatment; if you remain obstinately unrepentant and stubbornly resist to the end, you will surely reap what you sow, leaving a stench for ten thousand years. Comprehensively governing the country according to law is the general trend, how can you mantises try to stop a chariot or ants shake a tree? You think carefully, choose cautiously. 

黑夜岂能永蔽白日？阳光必将普照大地，尔等速速醒来！

Can the dark night forever cover the daylight? The sunlight will surely illuminate the earth, you wake up quickly!
