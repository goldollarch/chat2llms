### 1. **Overview of the Daubert Standard**

The Daubert standard, established by the U.S. Supreme Court in *Daubert v. Merrell Dow Pharmaceuticals* (1993), provides criteria for evaluating the admissibility of expert testimony and scientific evidence. It requires judges to act as gatekeepers, ensuring that evidence is:

- **Testable and falsifiable**
- **Peer-reviewed and published**
- **Known to have a low error rate**
- **Subject to standards and controls**
- **Generally accepted within the relevant scientific community**

These criteria aim to prevent pseudoscience and unreliable claims from influencing judicial outcomes.
