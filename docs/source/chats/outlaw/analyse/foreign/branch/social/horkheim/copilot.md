# Copilot

-------

**简要回答 / Brief Answer**  
从马克斯·霍克海默的批判理论视角看，本案体现了**工具理性取代批判理性**、法律被转化为维稳工具、以及社会公共领域的压缩。这种现象揭示了现代社会中理性沦为统治手段的危机。  
From Max Horkheimer’s critical theory perspective, this case illustrates the **replacement of critical reason by instrumental reason**, the transformation of law into a tool of control, and the shrinking of the public sphere. It reveals the crisis of reason becoming an instrument of domination in modern society.  

---

## 一、传统理论与批判理论  
### Traditional Theory vs. Critical Theory  

**中文**：  
霍克海默在《传统与批判理论》中区分了两种理性：传统理论追求中立与工具性，而批判理论则关注解放与社会变革。本案中，国家对“寻衅滋事”的运用体现了传统理论式的工具理性：法律被当作维持秩序的技术手段，而不是促进社会正义的批判工具。  

**English**:  
In *Traditional and Critical Theory*, Horkheimer distinguished between two forms of reason: traditional theory, which is neutral and instrumental, and critical theory, which aims at emancipation and social change. In this case, the state’s use of “picking quarrels” reflected traditional-theory-style instrumental reason: law was treated as a technical means of maintaining order, not as a critical tool for advancing justice.  

---

## 二、工具理性与理性的“蚀灭”  
### Instrumental Reason and the Eclipse of Reason  

**中文**：  
霍克海默在《理性的蚀灭》中指出，现代社会的理性逐渐沦为工具理性，只服务于统治与效率。在本案中，法律的功能被简化为“维稳”，而忽视了其应有的规范性与解放性。这正是理性被“蚀灭”的表现：它不再追求真理与自由，而是成为压制的手段。  

**English**:  
In *Eclipse of Reason*, Horkheimer argued that modern reason degenerates into instrumental reason, serving domination and efficiency. In this case, law’s function was reduced to “stability maintenance,” ignoring its normative and emancipatory role. This was precisely the eclipse of reason: no longer seeking truth and freedom, reason became a means of repression.  

---

## 三、权威与社会顺从  
### Authority and Social Conformity  

**中文**：  
霍克海默与阿多诺在《启蒙的辩证法》中分析了现代社会如何在启蒙名义下制造新的神话与权威。本案中，国家以“秩序”的名义压制异议，制造了“批判必然导致混乱”的神话。这种逻辑迫使社会成员顺从权威，削弱了公共理性的空间。  

**English**:  
Horkheimer and Adorno in *Dialectic of Enlightenment* analyzed how modern society, under the banner of enlightenment, creates new myths and authority. In this case, the state repressed dissent in the name of “order,” creating the myth that “criticism necessarily leads to chaos.” This logic forced social conformity to authority and eroded the space for public reason.  

---

## 四、批判理论的使命  
### The Mission of Critical Theory  

**中文**：  
霍克海默强调，批判理论的使命是揭示社会中的统治逻辑，并为解放开辟可能性。本案中，批判性言论本应成为社会自我反思与进步的契机，但却被压制。批判理论会提醒我们：只有通过承认异议与批判，社会才能避免理性的堕落，保持解放的可能。  

**English**:  
Horkheimer emphasized that the mission of critical theory is to expose the logic of domination in society and open possibilities for emancipation. In this case, critical speech should have been an opportunity for social self-reflection and progress but was suppressed. Critical theory would remind us that only by recognizing dissent and critique can society avoid the degeneration of reason and preserve the possibility of emancipation.  

---

## 五、结论  
### Conclusion  

**中文**：  
从霍克海默的批判理论视角看，本案揭示了：  
- 法律被工具化为维稳手段；  
- 理性沦为统治工具，出现“理性的蚀灭”；  
- 国家制造神话，迫使社会顺从；  
- 批判性言论的压制削弱了社会的解放潜能。  

**English**:  
From Horkheimer’s critical theory perspective, this case reveals that:  
- Law was instrumentalized as a tool of control;  
- Reason degenerated into domination, an “eclipse of reason”;  
- The state created myths to enforce conformity;  
- Suppression of critical speech weakened society’s emancipatory potential.
