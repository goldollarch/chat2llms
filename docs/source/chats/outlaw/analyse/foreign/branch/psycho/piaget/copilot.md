## 一、认知发展阶段与社会制度  
### Cognitive Development Stages and Social Institutions  

**中文**：  
皮亚杰认为，个体的认知发展经历感知运动、前运算、具体运算和形式运算阶段，最终达到抽象思维与道德推理的能力。在社会层面，制度也应当反映这种发展逻辑：一个成熟的社会应当允许公民运用“形式运算”阶段的能力，即通过抽象推理与批判性思考来参与公共事务。本案中，国家却将这种批判性表达视为威胁，实际上是阻碍了社会整体的“认知成熟”。  

**English**:  
Piaget argued that cognitive development progresses through sensorimotor, preoperational, concrete operational, and formal operational stages, culminating in abstract reasoning and moral judgment. At the societal level, institutions should reflect this logic: a mature society should allow citizens to exercise formal operational thinking—abstract reasoning and critical reflection in public life. In this case, however, the state treated such critical expression as a threat, effectively hindering the society’s cognitive maturity.  

---

## 二、同化与顺应的阻断  
### Blockage of Assimilation and Accommodation  

**中文**：  
皮亚杰提出，知识的发展依赖于“同化”与“顺应”的平衡：个体将新经验纳入既有框架（同化），并在必要时调整框架以适应新经验（顺应）。在本案中，公民的批判性言论本应成为社会“顺应”的契机，推动制度更新。然而，国家选择压制，拒绝顺应，从而维持僵化的认知框架。这种阻断使社会知识体系停滞，无法进化。  

**English**:  
Piaget proposed that knowledge develops through a balance of assimilation and accommodation: individuals assimilate new experiences into existing frameworks and accommodate by adjusting frameworks when necessary. In this case, citizens’ critical speech should have been an opportunity for societal accommodation, prompting institutional adaptation. Yet the state chose repression, refusing accommodation, thereby maintaining a rigid cognitive framework. This blockage left the social knowledge system stagnant and unable to evolve.  

---

## 三、个体与社会的建构主义  
### Constructivism of Individual and Society  

**中文**：  
皮亚杰的发生认识论强调，知识不是被动接受的，而是通过与环境互动主动建构的。在本案中，陈京元的表达正是这种“主动建构”的体现：他通过言论与社会现实互动，试图推动公共知识的生成。然而，国家的惩罚否认了这种建构过程，把知识简化为单向灌输，违背了建构主义的基本原则。  

**English**:  
Piaget’s genetic epistemology emphasized that knowledge is not passively received but actively constructed through interaction with the environment. In this case, Chen’s expression embodied such active construction: through speech, he interacted with social reality, attempting to generate public knowledge. Yet the state’s punishment denied this constructive process, reducing knowledge to one-way indoctrination, contradicting the principles of constructivism.  

---

## 四、道德发展与公民责任  
### Moral Development and Civic Responsibility  

**中文**：  
皮亚杰还研究了儿童的道德发展，指出从“他律道德”到“自律道德”的转变是成熟的标志。在本案中，国家要求公民绝对服从法律与权威，这是一种“他律道德”；而陈京元的行为体现了“自律道德”，即基于普遍原则与内在责任的行动。冲突的根源在于：制度停留在他律阶段，而个体已经进入自律阶段。  

**English**:  
Piaget also studied moral development, noting that the shift from “heteronomous morality” to “autonomous morality” marks maturity. In this case, the state demanded absolute obedience to law and authority, reflecting heteronomous morality; Chen’s actions reflected autonomous morality, acting on universal principles and inner responsibility. The root of conflict lies in the fact that institutions remained at the heteronomous stage while the individual had reached the autonomous stage.  

---

## 五、结论  
### Conclusion  

**中文**：  
从皮亚杰的发生认识论与认知发展理论视角看，陈京元案揭示了：  
- 社会制度阻碍了公民形式运算思维的发挥；  
- 国家拒绝“顺应”，导致认知框架僵化；  
- 惩罚否认了知识的建构性，强化了灌输；  
- 制度停留在“他律道德”，而个体已进入“自律道德”。  

皮亚杰会提醒我们：一个真正成熟的社会，应当像成熟的个体一样，能够通过顺应与建构不断发展，并承认公民的自主道德与批判性思维。  

**English**:  
From Piaget’s genetic epistemology and cognitive development theory perspective, Chen Jingyuan’s case reveals that:  
- Institutions hindered citizens’ exercise of formal operational thinking.  
- The state refused accommodation, leaving cognitive frameworks rigid.  
- Punishment denied the constructive nature of knowledge, reinforcing indoctrination.  
- Institutions remained at heteronomous morality, while the individual had reached autonomous morality.  

Piaget would remind us that a truly mature society, like a mature individual, must evolve through accommodation and construction, recognizing citizens’ autonomous morality and critical thinking.
