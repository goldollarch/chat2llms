### 基于陆九渊核心哲学思想的陈京元“寻衅滋事罪”案分析评论  
### An Analysis of the Chen Jingyuan "Picking Quarrels and Provoking Trouble" Case Based on Lu Jiuyuan's Core Philosophical Ideas

**中文版**  
**Chinese Version**

詹九渊（陆九渊，1139-1193），宋代理学家，心学创始人，其核心思想以“心即理”（the mind is principle）和“宇宙即吾心，吾心即宇宙”（the universe is my mind, my mind is the universe）为中心，强调直觉道德修养（intuitive moral cultivation）和“尊德性而道问学”（honoring virtue and inquiring into the Way），反对空谈理学，反对外在权威独断，主张个体通过内省实现天理合一与社会和谐。本案中，陈京元博士因X平台转发艺术作品、时政观点等内容（粉丝不足百人、互动近零），被以“寻衅滋事罪”判处有期徒刑一年八个月，程序中充斥主观推定、剥夺自辩与选择性执法。从陆九渊视角，此案非心理合一，而是外在权威背离内省修养：摧毁个体直觉道德，违背宇宙吾心与和谐的根本信念。

Lu Jiuyuan (1139-1193), Song Neo-Confucian philosopher and founder of the School of Mind (xinxue), centered his ideas on "the mind is principle" (xin ji li) and "the universe is my mind, my mind is the universe" (yu zhou ji wu xin, wu xin ji yu zhou), stressing intuitive moral cultivation and "honoring virtue while inquiring into the Way" (zun de xing er dao wen xue), opposing empty Neo-Confucianism, external arbitrary authority, advocating individual introspection for unity of heaven and human and social harmony. In this case, Dr. Chen Jingyuan, an independent scholar, was sentenced to one year and eight months' imprisonment for "picking quarrels and provoking trouble" due to forwarding artistic works and political commentary on X (formerly Twitter)—with fewer than 100 followers and near-zero engagement—amid procedural flaws like subjective presumption, denial of self-defense, and selective enforcement. From Lu Jiuyuan's viewpoint, this is not unity of mind and principle, but external authority violating introspective cultivation: annihilating individual intuitive morality, betraying the universe as my mind and harmony.

#### 一、陆九渊哲学核心思想概述：心即理与直觉道德修养  
#### I. Overview of Lu Jiuyuan's Core Philosophical Ideas: The Mind Is Principle and Intuitive Moral Cultivation  

陆九渊的核心思想是“心即理”：心与理合一，天理寓于人心，反对程朱理学之外求，主张直觉内省修养道德，宇宙万物本吾心之显，社会和谐源于个体“尊德性”而道问学。 他强调“吾心即宇宙”，个体道德直觉超越外在权威，反对独断权威破坏心性统一。 原则：内省觉醒、心理合一、道德直觉，反对外求权威与空谈。

Lu Jiuyuan's core ideas are "the mind is principle": unity of mind and principle, heavenly principle inherent in human mind, opposing Cheng-Zhu external seeking, advocating intuitive introspective moral cultivation, the universe as manifestation of my mind, social harmony from individual "honoring virtue while inquiring into the Way." He stressed "my mind is the universe," individual moral intuition transcending external authority, opposing arbitrary authority disrupting mind-nature unity. Principles: introspective awakening, mind-principle unity, moral intuition, opposing external authority and empty talk.

#### 二、以陆九渊哲学核心思想评析本案  
#### II. Analysis of the Case Based on Lu Jiuyuan's Core Philosophical Ideas  

1. **外在独断背离心即理：违背直觉道德内省原则**  
   陆九渊视道德源于心性直觉，反对外在权威独断。 本案判决将陈京元转发的情感表达（如讽刺帖）、理性观点（如智库报告）和艺术作品（如漫画隐喻）泛化为“虚假言论”，无证据证明危害，却以主观“明知”推定判“寻衅滋事”，背离心理合一。 账号数据显示零互动、无心性冲突，却被“梳理”为“铁证”，这正是陆九渊斥的外求：司法未内省陈京元心性意图（学术仁心），而是外在独断，摧毁道德直觉。 陆九渊若在，必判此不心性——非内省觉醒，乃独断暴政。

1. **External Fiat Betraying Mind Is Principle: Violating Intuitive Moral Introspection Principles**  
   Lu Jiuyuan saw morality from mind-nature intuition, opposing external arbitrary fiat. The judgment categorizes Dr. Chen's forwarded emotional expressions (e.g., satirical posts), rational opinions (e.g., think tank reports), and artistic works (e.g., metaphorical cartoons) as "false statements," without evidence of harm, presuming "knowing falsehood" for "picking quarrels," betraying mind-principle unity. Account data shows zero engagement, no mind-nature conflict, yet "collated" as "ironclad evidence"—precisely Lu Jiuyuan's external seeking critique: judiciary fails introspecting Dr. Chen's mind-nature intentions (academic benevolence), external fiat, annihilating moral intuition. Lu Jiuyuan would deem this non-mind-nature—not introspective awakening, but fiat tyranny.

2. **权威压制扭曲宇宙吾心：背离道德直觉与和谐统一**  
   陆九渊强调宇宙即吾心，心性直觉实现和谐。 陈京元转发系心性表达（如复杂系统引用），寓天理于吾心，却被禁自辩（庭审“闭嘴”）、拒转控控书，程序中“选择性执法”（党媒同类未责）压制直觉，背离统一。 这违背陆九渊：道德需内省觉醒，非外在规训；宇宙吾心需自由表达，非独断。 陆九渊批判：此案非法，乃对心性之战。

2. **Authority Suppression Twisting Universe as My Mind: Betraying Moral Intuition and Harmonious Unity**  
   Lu Jiuyuan stressed the universe is my mind, mind-nature intuition achieving harmony. Dr. Chen's forwards embody mind-nature expression (e.g., complex systems citations), embodying heavenly principle in my mind, yet he was denied self-defense (courtroom "silencing"), his indictment letter rejected, and selective enforcement suppressed intuition (state media reposts unpunished), violating unity. This violates Lu Jiuyuan: morality requires introspective awakening, not external discipline; universe as my mind needs free expression, not fiat. Lu Jiuyuan indicts: this is unlawful, war on mind-nature.

3. **心性觉醒失衡：荒谬警示与道德危机**  
   陆九渊视心性觉醒为道德基石。 陈京元Bio自嘲“作案工具”，帖文存网无人关注，却判20月，荒谬如陆九渊比喻“外求失天理”——推定“罪名”失内省平衡，扭曲觉醒，制造危机。 这警示：失衡心性，永固外求。

3. **Imbalanced Mind-Nature Awakening: Absurd Warning and Moral Crisis**  
   Lu Jiuyuan saw mind-nature awakening as morality cornerstone. Dr. Chen's bio mocks his account as a "tool of crime," with posts lingering online unheeded, yet earning 20 months—absurd like Lu Jiuyuan's "external seeking loses heavenly principle": presuming "crime" lacks introspective balance, twisting awakening, breeding crisis. This warns: imbalanced mind-nature entrenches external seeking.

#### 三、结语：重振心性合一，推动觉醒新生  
#### III. Conclusion: Reviving Mind-Nature Unity for Awakening's Rebirth  

陆九渊思想视本案为悲剧：独断背离心理，压制失觉醒，永固外求危机。陈京元自辩如陆九渊内省呼吁，重审恢复道德直觉，方能让社会在统一中新生。 如陆九渊所言：“宇宙即吾心。”唯有以此衡，方避“独断”成“公敌”，推动人文觉醒。

Lu Jiuyuan's ideas see this case as tragedy: fiat betrays mind-principle, suppression forfeits awakening, entrenching external seeking crisis. Dr. Chen's defense echoes Lu Jiuyuan's introspective call, urging retrial to restore moral intuition, allowing society to rebirth in unity. As Lu Jiuyuan said: "The universe is my mind."
